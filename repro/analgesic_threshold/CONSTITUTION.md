# Constitution — Free Analgesic-Logic Proposal (draft for Phase-2 M0)

This constitution governs the `analgesic_threshold_logic` whitepaper. It is written to be
folded into the Phase-2 build and gate-checked for presence (string-gate).

## Purpose
To offer, **free of charge and openly**, a DNA-grounded *threshold-engineering map* and an
*intervention logic* for non-opioid analgesia — i.e. **ideas and candidate directions** that
pharmaceutical companies and academic researchers may use to develop cheaper, safer pain
medicines, ultimately to reduce the suffering of patients whom current analgesics fail. The
work exists because countless people are harmed by — or unhelped by — existing painkillers.

## What this work IS
- A reproducible read of the nociceptor's firing-threshold architecture from real promoter DNA.
- A ranked map of molecular *targets* and a *direction* of intervention (raise the firing
  threshold / restore the OFF basin, selectively).
- An explicit, falsifiable **hypothesis** offered as a starting point for proper R&D.

## What this work IS NOT (binding limits)
- **Not medical advice and not a treatment.** Nothing here diagnoses, treats, cures, or
  prevents any condition. No patient should act on it.
- **Not a drug.** No molecule is designed; **no synthesis, formulation, or manufacturing
  route is provided**; no dose, regimen, or route of administration is given.
- **Not a clinical or safety claim.** No efficacy, safety, potency, or pharmacokinetic result
  is asserted. Such results can come only from laboratory and clinical validation, which this
  work does not perform.
- **Not a medical responsibility.** The author assumes **no medical, clinical, or liability
  responsibility** of any kind. Any party choosing to pursue these ideas does so under their
  own scientific, ethical, and regulatory responsibility.

## Distribution & licence
- **Free distribution**, published under **CC BY 4.0**, with a **DOI** (Zenodo), consistent
  with the rest of the jamming-physics programme. ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245.
- Reuse, including commercial reuse by pharmaceutical companies, is **permitted and
  encouraged** — that is the point: to lower the cost and broaden the search for better
  analgesics. Attribution as per CC BY 4.0.

## Public-benefit and good-faith statement
This is a good-faith, public-benefit contribution. **Even if the hypothesis turns out to be
wrong, it is not irresponsible**, because it is shared freely, labelled plainly as an
untested hypothesis carrying no medical responsibility, and steered deliberately toward
*non-addictive, peripheral* targets and away from the opioids that have harmed so many. If it
helps even indirectly — by giving one team a cheaper path to one better analgesic — it serves
the future of patients.

## Methodological discipline (inherited)
- **No tuning:** every constant is a measured input or derived from it; nothing is chosen to
  fit a target.
- **Honest grading:** `[V]` verified / `[F]` forced / `[O]` open, with every open item in the
  ledger; bit-reproducible; fail-closed gates.
- **Axis-A(analgesic) firewall:** *threshold structure we can read* is held strictly separate
  from *clinical effect we cannot assert*. The build fails closed if that line is crossed.
