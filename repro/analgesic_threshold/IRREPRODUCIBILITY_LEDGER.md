# Irreproducibility Ledger — analgesic_threshold_logic v2.0

Every open item is listed here and is **never** asserted as evidence anywhere in the work.
Grades: `[V]` verified/reproducible · `[F]` forced by the reads · `[O]` open (needs external data).

v2.0 expands the target set from 7 to **27** (5 inherited + 22 fetched), adds the **three-lever**
frame (L1 reduce inward current · L2 increase K⁺ outward · L3 remove sensitising drive), a
**burden-weighted target prioritisation**, an **L3-honesty gate**, and a **precision local-anaesthesia**
map. None of these changes the firewall: γ reads promoter switch-threshold **structure**; every
clinical magnitude remains `[O]`.

## What is `[V]` / `[F]` (closed)
- **γ reads of all 27 targets** — `[V]`. Bit-reproducible from human promoters (SantaLucia 1998 NN
  table, TSS−2000..+500). The 22 fetched targets re-derive offline from the bundled NCBI cache;
  the harness proves drift 0 over three runs and runs with the network disabled.
- **R19 threshold scale** (|h_sp| = (2/3√3)γ^1.5, barrier γ²/4) for each target — `[V]`
  (locked engine forms, unchanged from v1.0).
- **corr(γ,GC) = 0.99898** over the full 27-target set — `[V]` (the near-mechanical γ↔GC relation
  of the SantaLucia table; not tuned, holds for any gene set).
- **The ordering** of the 27 targets by |h_sp| — `[F]` (forced by the reads, not chosen).
- **Three-lever classification, L1 and L2** (which channel/current each target gates, and the push
  direction) — `[F]` structural, anchored to cited agents (e.g. suzetrigine/Na_V1.8 L1, the approved
  K_V7 opener arm L2). The *gene's place* in the lever map is forced by its γ read; the *direction*
  is the cited Layer-2 biology.
- **Precision-block mechanism SHAPE** (nociceptor-selective entry port × charged firing-threshold
  raiser → differential block) — `[F]` structural, anchored to Binshtok, Bean & Woolf (Nature 2007).
- **The direction of intervention** (raise threshold / restore OFF basin) — `[F]`, anchored to
  measured SCN9A/NTRK1 channelopathy phenotypes and the approved Na_V1.8 closed-state mechanism.

## What is `[O]` (open — asserted nowhere)
1. **All absolute thresholds** (firing, mechanical, thermal beyond the cited TRPV1 ~43 °C order).
2. **The channels' biophysical activation/inactivation voltages.** γ is a promoter-stiffness read,
   **not** a channel voltage, potency, or dose; the map never equates them.
3. **In-vivo nociceptor selectivity** of any effector across all 27 targets (the selectivity tiers
   are cited Layer-2 literature, not engine outputs).
4. **Any clinical quantity** — potency, dose, efficacy, safety, pharmacokinetics, liability profile —
   for **any** candidate at **any** of the 27 targets. The framework does not and cannot produce these.
5. **Whether cognition/behaviour-level pain relief follows** from a given threshold shift — outside
   scope; only the molecular threshold structure is read.
6. **The mapping γ → expression-switch behaviour** at these loci (named as the framework-level
   falsifier; not assumed).
7. **The L3 receptor/network mechanism link.** For every L3 target (NGF, NTRK1, CALCA, CALCB,
   CALCRL, RAMP1), γ only places the gene in the lever map; the ligand→receptor→sensitisation
   mechanism is **cited biology, never derived** by the read. Enforced fail-closed by the L3-honesty
   gate (11-l3-honesty).
8. **The burden-prioritisation weights** (w_B = 0.40 burden, w_U = 0.35 unmet need, w_D = 0.25
   druggability) and the per-target B/U/D tiers are a **declared editorial choice with cited
   one-line justifications**, not a derived result. The priority score ranks **targets/reads**, never
   drugs, doses, or efficacy, and the γ-|h_sp| "map place" is carried **alongside** but is **never
   folded into** the clinical priority score (firewall: promoter stiffness ≠ clinical magnitude).
9. **The precision differential-block magnitudes** — differential-block ratio, duration, toxicity,
   formulation, concentration, route, and dose for any entry-port × blocker pairing. The map states
   the differential-block *property* as a **prediction**, never a measured result. The cited
   charged-blocker tool compounds (QX-314, chloroprocaine) are **experimental anchors, not proposed
   therapeutics** (parallel to suzetrigine being named only as the realised L1 anchor).
