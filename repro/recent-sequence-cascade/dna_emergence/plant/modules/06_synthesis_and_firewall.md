**What the plant material says.**

1. **Plastid core material is conserved within and across kinds (Module 02, [V]).** Very different-looking plants in different habitats share the same core gene material — the user's "겉모양 달라도 유전자 비슷" holds for the conserved core. (Honest: no strong two-kind partition, unlike animal mtDNA.)
2. **The clock is decoupled from the material in plants too (Module 03, [V]).** Independent-kingdom confirmation that the average theory's deep-time axis is a different channel from the material.
3. **The cold-vs-UV difference is STATE/inventory, not material (Module 04, [V]).** The environment deploys a different switch (CBF vs CHS/UVR8) and sets its dwell; γ is a lineage property (Module 05) the environment does not rewrite.

**The firewall.** Everything above is a **present-tense material measurement**. Any statement that a particular **past environment or history occurred** — "a past cold greenhouse," a flood, a deep-time descent — is capped at **[O] (open)** in both directions. The result that the environment does not write the material is a statement about *where the environment acts*, not about *what history happened*.

**Forward predictions (falsifiable).**
- **DP1 [L]** — for any stress gene across lineages, γ tracks lineage GC, not the stress environment. *Fails if* an environmental axis sorts γ after GC control.
- **DP2 [L]** — within one lineage, cold- and UV-switch genes read on one material scale. *Fails if* they separate materially after GC control.
- **DP3 [L]** — cold vs UV adaptation maps to *which* loci are deployed (inventory) and their dwell, not a γ shift of a shared locus. *Fails if* a shared locus shifts materially between cold- and UV-adapted lineages.
- **DP4 [L]** — clock decoupling holds out-of-sample (r ≈ 0 on length-matched windows). *Fails if* substitutions predict |Δγ| (|r| ≳ 0.5).
- **DP5 [F-leaning]** — γ stays a near-deterministic function of GC across plant loci. *Fails if* γ and GC decouple on clean coding windows.
