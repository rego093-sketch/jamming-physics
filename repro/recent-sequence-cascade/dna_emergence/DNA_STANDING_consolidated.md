## 1. What the DNA establishes (present-tense, [V] — Module 22)

1. **Material γ is a lineage/kind property, not time and not environment.** Animal: mammoth/elephant and Neanderthal/human each read as one material kind (clean partition). Plant: plastid core machinery is conserved across grasses and nightshades (band centres 1.2821 vs 1.2809). γ is a composition measure: r(γ, GC) = 0.98–0.998.
2. **The molecular clock is decoupled from the material.** r(substitutions, |Δγ|) ≈ −0.08 (animal, n=78), +0.09 (plant, n=372). A clock count is neither elapsed time nor material.
3. **Environment writes to STATE, not material.** Cold (CBF/DREB1) and UV (CHS, UVR8) are different loci; CHS γ tracks lineage (grass vs not), not habitat.

## 2. The structural verdict — DNA is neutral on the occurrence, both directions (Art. 2)

Because material is **decoupled from time** (fact 2) and **conserved regardless of kind/time** (fact 1):

- A small |Δγ| is **consistent with any elapsed time** — it discriminates nothing about duration. So DNA **cannot** be promoted to evidence for a compressed timeline (a flood), and it equally **dissolves** "DNA divergence proves deep time" (molecular-clock dating). DNA exits the elapsed-time question **in both directions**.
- The occurrence and any absolute timescale are decided — if at all — in the **history channel** (stratigraphy, sedimentology, dated deposits), never by γ.

## 3. Placement in the causal chain

The cascade's load-bearing causal chain is physical: **S0 → H-F → H-G → H-A → S4** (mechanisms [F]/[V], occurrence [O]). DNA is **not** a node in that chain; it is a **biology↔history bridge** that bounds what biology may claim about it. Its role is *protective and symmetric*: it removes a standard objection to recency without becoming a positive argument for it.

## 4. Tie-in to Module 26 (taxa / contamination)

This session asked whether the fauna changed at 2300 BC (mammoth/ancient vs elephant/modern). The DNA answer is precise and firewall-clean:

- DNA says **mammoth ≠ elephant** and **Neanderthal ≠ modern human** as **material kinds (lineages)** — a clean present-tense partition (fact 1). [V].
- But DNA is **neutral on *when*** those lineages diverged or coexisted (fact 2). So DNA **cannot date the turnover** in either direction — it neither makes mammoth "ancient" nor "recent".
- Therefore the 2300 BC turnover question routes entirely to the **history/record channel** (Module 26), where the radiocarbon counts are firewalled (DISCOVERED ≠ EXISTING; dates RECORD) and the elevation discriminator failed. DNA and the radiocarbon record **agree on one thing: neither can settle the chronology**, by construction.
