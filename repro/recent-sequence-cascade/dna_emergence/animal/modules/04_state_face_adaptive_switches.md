> **One line.** If the material is conserved within a kind, where does a real adaptive difference live? In **STATE.** On the two best-known adaptive loci — cold haemoglobin and the coat-colour switch — the inherited engine reads **conserved material** with the difference confined to **a handful of coding substitutions**, and *recovers the exact amino-acid changes the literature reports.* MC1R is caught as one R19 bistable with **both states present in the same kind**.

## 1. STATE face 1 — HBB/D, cold-tolerant haemoglobin (Campbell et al. 2010)

Real accessions: mammoth FJ716094.1, Asian elephant FJ716086.1, African elephant NM_001280882.1 (all 444 bp CDS).

- **Material conserved:** γ range across the three = **0.0052**.
- **mammoth vs Asian:** 4 nt → **3 amino-acid changes: A13T, S87A, Q102E** — *exactly* the cold-adaptation substitutions Campbell et al. identified as conferring the mammoth's reduced O₂-release temperature sensitivity.

The adaptive difference is a localized coding edit on a materially conserved locus — a switch STATE, not a material shift. The engine was not told the answer; it reproduced it.

## 2. STATE face 2 — MC1R, the coat-colour switch, caught in both states (Rømpler et al. 2006)

Real accessions: mammoth haplotype 1 DQ648860.1, mammoth haplotype 2 DQ648859.1, Asian elephant DQ648866.1 (all 954 bp CDS).

- **Material conserved:** γ range across the three = **0.0023**.
- **mammoth hap1 vs hap2 (the coat switch):** 3 nt → **3 amino-acid changes: T21A, R67C, R301S**, |Δγ| = **0.0019** — material identical, only the **STATE** differs.

This is **one R19 bistable switch with its two settings both present in the same kind**: a full-activity coat state and a reduced-activity coat state (Rømpler et al. inferred a light/dark coat polymorphism in mammoths from exactly this). One switch, one conserved material, two coat states — the foundation's universal bistable, observed directly.

## 3. Why this is the predicted picture

The inherited thesis says the difference the environment sets and remembers lives in **inventory / state / dwell**, not in the material. The STATE face confirms it on adaptive loci: the material (γ) is held; the adaptation is a small, localized, *switchable* change — and where the phenotype is switch-like, the alternatives read as two states of one bistable. This is the same R19 switch that γ's threshold is built from (Module 01), now seen in the act.
