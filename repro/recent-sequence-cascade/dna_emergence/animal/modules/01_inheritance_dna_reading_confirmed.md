## 1. What the inherited method is

The inherited DNA method reads any locus deterministically into measured channels. The load-bearing one for this volume is the **material channel**:

- **γ = −mean(NN stacking ΔG)** over the SantaLucia (1998) unified nearest-neighbour table (kcal/mol), strand-symmetric, bit-for-bit.
- From γ alone, the locus's **R19 bistable switch** scale: spinodal = (2/3√3)·γ^1.5, barrier = γ²/4. No further input.

The method is **explicitly evolution-silent and phylogeny-out-of-scope**: it reads *present-tense material*, never descent or age. This is not a limitation we impose; it is the method's own stated scope — and it is precisely the property that makes it an instrument capable of *doubting the average theory* (it never reads a sequence as a point on an averaged descent-gradient).
