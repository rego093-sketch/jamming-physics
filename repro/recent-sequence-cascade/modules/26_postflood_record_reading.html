Module 26 — Reading the post-flood record: taxa, radiocarbon contamination, and the elevation discriminator

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: biology↔history bridge (extends Module 23) · Inherits: Constitution Art. 2 (two-edged firewall), Art. 4 (occurrence/absolute-date capped [O] / RECORD); governance/00 (D0–D5). Data: p3k14c (Bird et al. 2022, public scrubbed/fuzzed cut), frozen subsets under repro/data/.
Status: EVIDENCE + DISCRIMINATOR module. Records a falsifiable test that the author proposed and that the raw data did not support, plus the firewall-neutral contamination arithmetic. Reproducible: three scripts under repro/, each with a double-SHA-256 gate.
Standing grade: raw counts [V]; all ¹⁴C ages D5 / RECORD (both directions); the elevation discriminator [F] (failed/reversed); the reduced reservoir mechanism [V] but non-load-bearing; the flood-chronology reading [O].

One line. Three honest results. (1) The radiocarbon count/taxa record cannot be promoted into a faunal-existence claim — p3k14c is archaeological, not palaeontological (DISCOVERED ≠ EXISTING), the human skeletal records are redacted, and the one abundant animal taxon (bison) is continuous across 2300 BC. (2) The author's elevation discriminator (a genuine falsifiable test) failed: within-region it reverses (Europe/N.America high = older, opposite to prediction), and the global pattern that superficially matches is a Simpson artifact. (3) The contamination arithmetic (firewall-neutral) shows water can shift dates older only via dead carbon and only by hundreds–~2000 yr in carbonate/aquatic settings — never a blanket +7000 yr — while modern-carbon contamination pushes old samples younger. Net: the flood-contamination reading survives only as a small, sample-specific, non-load-bearing correction [O]; absolute ¹⁴C dates remain RECORD, both directions.

A. The contamination arithmetic — firewall-neutral (repro/contamination_arithmetic.py)

This is instrument physics, true under any chronology (it imports no past-occurrence model). With F_meas = (1-c)·F_true + c·F_contam, age = -8033·ln F:

  • Dead carbon (F=0) → OLDER, shift = -8033·ln(1-c), independent of true age: c=5% → +412 yr; c=10% → +846 yr; c≈58% → +7000 yr. To inflate a 4 ka sample to 11 ka requires the sample to be ~58 % dead carbon — not subtle contamination but near-total replacement, flagged by collagen ultrafiltration + C:N screening.
  • Modern carbon (F=1) → YOUNGER, and old samples are hypersensitive: a true 40,000 BP sample dates to ~33,000 BP at just 1 % modern carbon, ~23,000 BP at 5 %. So contamination's stronger tendency is to drag old samples toward the present, opposite to "everything pre-flood reads older."

Gate: 2×SHA-256 = faabe48d95a0ad9f432b388d51442fc7bd333f9cf1735c5fd7ab070ac283092b.

B. Taxa composition — raw counts only (repro/taxa_composition.py)

Over the frozen subset (675 records; ages are RECORD, used only as recorded values):

  • Homo sapiens / human: 202 — but the public cut redacts skeletal records; these are coprolite-dominated (DISCOVERED ≠ EXISTING with a third layer, redaction R(t,place)). Present on both sides of 2300 BC, but not a clean test.
  • Mammuthus: 42 (mostly one N.America collection) — ages cluster old (median ≈ 10,800 ¹⁴C BP), a few young outliers.
  • Mammut/mastodon: ~3 — all ≈ 9,600–11,400 ¹⁴C BP.
  • Bison: 425 — ages span the whole range continuously (≈ 65–11,990 ¹⁴C BP), present before and after 2300 BC.
  • Elephas/Loxodonta: 0 — a geographic sampling artifact (DB is Americas/Europe-weighted), not a temporal statement.

Reading (firewall-clean). A faunal turnover localised at 2300 BC is not present in the raw counts: bison is continuous across it (a direct counterexample to a universal die-off), and the one taxon that drops out (mammoth) does so at a different ¹⁴C position. Whether the mammoth old-cluster reflects real antiquity, the author's contamination inflation, or sampling — and where it maps under a compressed chronology — is [O], since the ages are RECORD both ways. Gate: 39cd3b8e1a712fe7f6801362fa034f72aa6c81d8e87ce80e56ca625581a237bd.

C. The elevation discriminator — a falsifiable test that FAILED (repro/elevation_test.py)

The author proposed a genuine, falsifiable prediction: apparent ¹⁴C age should anti-correlate with elevation (high = young, low = old), within a region (caves/dry refuge → less water contamination). Tested on a SEED=19 sample (n = 5,444) with Copernicus DEM elevation:

  • Within-region (the requested control): Europe Spearman(elev, age) = +0.214, North America +0.172 — i.e. high = OLDER, the opposite of the prediction (t ≈ 10, 8). Asia/Africa ≈ 0; South America −0.07 (weak, prediction direction, not significant); Australia ≈ 0. Global ≈ −0.03.
  • Simpson trap: the global elevation bands superficially match the prediction (0–200 m: med ≈ 3140 BP → 1000–2000 m: ≈ 2315 BP), but this is a region-mix artifact — applying the same-region control the author demanded reverses it.

Reading (honest, both directions). This is not a clean kill: the coordinates are fuzzed/county-aggregated (elevation noise attenuates correlations), and the observed "high = older" is itself the expected settlement pattern (younger lowland agriculture), which is only [O]. So the discriminator does not mechanistically refute the author — it simply fails to support the direction and, where testable, runs opposite. Elevation is a poor proxy for the true contamination driver. Gate: cc9db07edf24be97d664e254f2a54d2ad5ddf41e5a22b8a7d2e1dc64a3e07e60.

D. What survives, and at what grade

Documented reservoir/hard-water effects are real and go the author's direction (older), but their magnitude is hundreds to ~2000 ¹⁴C yr, confined to carbonate/hard-water settings and aquatic-diet organisms (detected by δ¹³C/δ¹⁵N), and modern AMS pretreatment removes non-reservoir contamination per sample. So the flood-contamination reading is not discarded — it survives as a small, sample-specific, non-load-bearing correction:

  • Mechanism ("water/carbonate exposure inflates a sample older; avoiding that water reduces it"): [V] (present-tense, documented — living aquatic organisms date old).
  • The author's careful claim ("human samples may be less contaminated by avoiding water"): [O] — not discarded, reasonable.
  • The over-scoped versions (elevation proxy / blanket +7000 yr / uniform older): [F] (direction, magnitude, and proxy all fail).
  • As load-bearing for the flood chronology: no — the surviving correction is small and sample-specific; absolute ¹⁴C dates stay RECORD, both directions (Art. 4).

E. The right test, deferred

The correct discriminator is not elevation but carbonate/hard-water context, aquatic vs terrestrial diet (δ¹⁵N), and collagen quality (C:N) — these read the reservoir effect directly. This archaeological DB lacks those sample-level fields, so the question stays [O] here; it needs sample-level data carrying context + isotopes + quality indicators.
