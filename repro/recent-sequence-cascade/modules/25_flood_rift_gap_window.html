One line. The flood (H-F) and the Atlantic expansion (H-A) are not simultaneous: H-A begins at least 100 yr after H-F. This non-immediacy is not assumed — it is entailed by the finite observed snowfall rate (you cannot load the crust instantly). The window is bounded: a firm author floor (100 yr) and a finite ceiling (the chain stays one relaxation only while the flood's heat fuels the snow engine), whose number inherits the flood budget = [O]. Floor and ceiling together restore a binding co-constraint: gap-duration and trigger-load are locked by one snow engine and funded by one flood budget. Occurrence and every absolute date stay [O], firewalled both ways.

2. Non-immediacy is entailed, not assumed — [F]

Observed maritime snowfall is 1–4 m w.e./yr (Tier-A D0/D1). The rate is finite, so instantaneous crustal loading is physically impossible. The minimum gap to build even the smallest near-critical trigger (~1 MPa) is, from repro/gap_window.py:

  • σ = 1 MPa → 25–102 yr (4 → 1 m/yr)
  • σ = 4 MPa → 102–408 yr · σ = 9 MPa → 229–917 yr · σ = 27 MPa → 688–2752 yr

So gap > 0 follows directly from the finite snowfall rate, and the author floor of ≥100 yr sits at the conservative (1 m/yr) end of the rate-limited minimum for a ~1 MPa trigger. "Flood, then a wait, then rupture" is mechanism, not narrative.

3. What the floor mechanically guarantees — [F]

Floor + the snow engine forces a minimum accumulated load by 100 yr (recomputed, not asserted):

  • 1 m/yr → 109 m ice → 0.98 MPa · 4 m/yr → 436 m ice → 3.92 MPa

By the time 100 yr have elapsed, ~1–4 MPa of basal load is already present — the near-critical-trigger regime. The floor therefore delivers its own trigger budget.

4. The ceiling is finite — but its number is [O]

The chain is one relaxation of a loaded metastable state (Blueprint Part 1), not five independent events. It remains "one" only while the flood's latent heat still drives the ocean-warming → snow engine. Once that fuel is spent the engine stops and any further loading would need a different driver — breaking the coupling. Hence a finite ceiling:

τ_fuel = Q_flood / (acc · ρ_w · L_vap)

The extraction rate acc · L_vap is Tier-A (the snow engine removes latent heat at 79–317 W/m² for 1–4 m/yr). But Q_flood is the flood/reservoir budget = SH-5 = [O] (HOLD). Therefore τ_fuel is finite by construction but numerically [O]; pinning it would be an import/overclaim and is withheld. (Raising it from [O] is exactly the SH-5 gate.)

5. The restored binding constraint — a co-constraint (one engine, one budget)

This replaces the prior, withdrawn "100 yr forces SH-10" (that was a ceiling property, void under a floor-only spec). The new, stronger constraint:

  • One snow engine locks duration to load: σ(MPa) = 9.81×10⁻³ · acc · τ. Duration and trigger-stress are not independent.
  • One flood budget funds both the engine's run-time and the ice it deposits (the same evaporated water that cools the ocean is the snow that loads the crust).

So gap and trigger-load are co-constrained by a single parameter — the framework's distinctive one-parameter signature (Module 18 P5 / SH-20 flavour). Clean test: does one flood budget jointly satisfy (a) ≥100 yr of fuel and (b) a trigger-sufficient load? It is decidable once SH-5 is pinned; today it is [O] because the budget is [O].

6. Consilience with Module 12 (Nile / H-F) — the compatibility, firewall-clean

The author's requirement that this be compatible with the Nile record holds by construction, and adds no date:

  • Orthogonality. The GAP is a relative duration; Module 12's Nile absolute ages (Eonile incision; Zanclean ~5.33 Ma) are D5 = RECORD, firewalled both directions. A duration cannot conflict with held-out absolute ages — the window neither uses nor contradicts any Nile date.
  • The Nile anchors the clock's t = 0. Module 12 reads H-F as a discrete, present-tense flood — extreme incision (Eonile canyon, D0/D1) + a catastrophic, rapid refill (Zanclean channel, D0/D1), preceded by a desiccation/loading phase (Messinian salt, D1). A sharp, completed event is precisely the discrete trigger the gap window needs to start at t = 0; the Nile shows the flood not smeared over deep time but as a finished pulse.
  • It refines the F↔A coupling, consistently. Module 12's coupling row — "flood and Atlantic event are two faces of one destabilisation", graded [O] — did not assert simultaneity. Module 25 refines it: the two faces are sequential (F then A), separated by ≥100 yr, the flood the Nile-observed earlier face. Refinement, not contradiction.
  • Firewall. No Nile date enters as load-bearing. The compatibility is structural (relative duration vs. held-out absolute age), never chronological.
