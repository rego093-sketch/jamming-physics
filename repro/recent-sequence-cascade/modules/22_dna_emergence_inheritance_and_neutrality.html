Module 22 — DNA Emergence inherited whole and runnable, and why DNA is neutral on the occurrence

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: biology↔history bridge · Inherits: VP DNA interpretation method (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20471407); the animal DNA-Emergence (mammoth/elephant, Neanderthal/human) and the plant DNA-Emergence (cold vs UV), both carried runnable under ../dna_emergence/. Consumes: cascade Modules 12–21.
Status: INHERITANCE + INTEGRATION. Per author directive ("opening this whitepaper, DNA emergence must actually be runnable, so inherit it whole, for verification"). Both sub-packages reproduce from frozen NCBI records, no network, no tuning.
Standing grade: present-tense material [V]; occurrence/timescale [O] in both directions.

One line. The cascade now carries the DNA Emergence as a runnable component, not a citation. The DNA work establishes three present-tense facts in two kingdoms — material is a lineage property, the clock is decoupled from the material, the environment writes to STATE not material — and those same facts make DNA neutral on whether any quiet interval was long or short. DNA removes the standard "sequence proves deep time" objection and refuses to be promoted to positive flood evidence. Symmetrically.

1. The inheritance is complete and runnable

../dna_emergence/animal/ and ../dna_emergence/plant/ are full packages (whitepaper, governance, reproducibility map, manifest, modules, repro spine). Each runs from frozen NCBI GenBank records with repro/run_all.py, no network, no fitted parameter (SEED = 19, SantaLucia 1998 LOCK). repro/fetch_ncbi.py re-fetches the raw sequences live. The reader can reproduce every number, which is the point of carrying them here.

2. What the DNA emergence measures (present-tense, [V])

  1. Material (γ) is a lineage/kind property, not an environment property. Animal: mammoth/elephant and Neanderthal/human each read as one material kind (clean partition). Plant: the plastid core machinery is conserved across grasses and nightshades (band centres 1.2821 vs 1.2809; rbcL range 0.0082 across six habitats).
  2. The molecular clock is decoupled from the material. r(substitutions, |Δγ|) ≈ −0.08 (animal, n = 78) and +0.09 (plant, n = 372). A clock count cannot be read as elapsed time or as material.
  3. The environment writes to STATE / inventory / dwell, not to the material. Cold (CBF/DREB1) and UV (CHS, UVR8) are different loci; CHS γ tracks lineage (cold barley and warm maize both high because both grasses), not habitat. r(γ, GC) = 0.98–0.998 — γ is a composition measure, which is why it is a lineage signature the environment cannot rewrite.

3. The firewall link — DNA does not decide the occurrence, in either direction

The cascade holds the flood occurrence and all absolute timescale [O] (Art. 1–2). The DNA emergence is fully consistent with that and tightens it:

  • Because material is decoupled from time (fact 2) and conserved regardless of kind/time (fact 1), a small sequence difference is observed even across mainstream-large divergences (mammoth/elephant). So a small |Δγ| is consistent with any elapsed time and discriminates nothing about duration.
  • Therefore DNA is neutral on whether a quiet interval was long or short. It cannot be promoted to positive evidence for a compressed timeline (a flood), and it equally dissolves the opposing "DNA divergence proves deep time" (molecular-clock dating). DNA exits the elapsed-time question in both directions.
  • This is the two-edged firewall (Art. 2) applied to sequence: the material/clock channel is removed from the time debate, leaving the duration of any quiet interval genuinely open. The flood, like deep time, is decided — if at all — in the history channel (stratigraphy, sedimentology, dated deposits), never by γ.

4. Standing

The DNA emergence is inherited whole and verified. Its present-tense reads are [V]; it adds no occurrence or timescale claim, and it explicitly refuses to let sequence conservation argue for or against a flood. That refusal — symmetric, recorded — is what keeps the cascade's [O] honest where biology meets history.

Grade: [V] for the inherited present-tense reads; [O] for occurrence/timescale, both directions, exactly as the cascade requires.
