Module 35 — GIA geometry and the strandline: an elastic ice-load signature (timing superseded by M44)

In plain terms. When a heavy ice sheet sits on the crust, it bends it down and pushes up a bulge just outside the ice; raised beaches mark where the old shoreline was. Those shapes are real and measurable today, and they confirm a big ice load existed — but the geometry by itself does not say which framework is right. One thing this module got too cautious about — when the ice melted — is fixed by Module 44, which shows the rebound is still going on now.
