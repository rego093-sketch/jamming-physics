Module 13 — C1-quant NoGo Screen: Physical Permission of Rapid Warm-Ocean Glaciation (H-F → H-G)

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: 1 (Recent-Sequence applications branch) · Inherits: Constitution & Blueprint (governance/01), Data Grading Charter (governance/00), C1 mechanism evidence (governance/07), Module 12 (S0/H-F anchors), Master Scenario (SCENARIO_recent_sequence.md §S2).
Status: GATE module. Runs the deferred quantitative NoGo gate C1-quant (SCENARIO §S2: "the continental-scaling quantitative gate, deferred"). Computational; reproducible.
Standing grade: the screen promotes only physical permission of mechanism / rate-capability (Constitution Art. 4.3, Art. 5). Occurrence of any past glaciation remains [O] (Art. 4.2).
Code: c1_quant_screen.py (SEED=19; no RNG exercised; no fitted parameter).

What this module does, in one line. governance/07 grounded C1's mechanism in present-tense physics (Clausius–Clapeyron + observed lake/sea-effect snow). This module runs the deferred quantitative gate: does building a continental ice sheet from a warm-ocean/cold-sky engine require physically absurd (STOP) parameters, or is it permitted — using only measured constants, observed snowfall rates, and present-day measured ice volumes, with no chronology, no reconstructed ice volume, no paleoclimate model.

0. The firewall as the screen's operating rule (bidirectional — Art. 3)

A quantitative gate is where "do not be swayed by the average theory" is most easily violated, so the admissibility rule is stated up front:

  • Admissible inputs (used): lab-measured thermodynamic constants (Lᵥ, L_f, c_p, ρ); observed present-day maritime snowfall rates (m water-equivalent / yr); present-day measured ice-sheet volumes and thickness (Greenland, Antarctica). All present-tense (Art. 2.1).
  • Forbidden inputs (NOT used), in both directions: any absolute chronology — in particular the mainstream "ice ages are paced at ~100 kyr" is not used as a benchmark the fast scenario must beat (that is chronology, governance/11 M3); reconstructed LGM ice volumes (model products); any paleoclimate reconstruction.
  • Not load-bearing: no fitted parameter. Every number is a measured constant or an explicitly-ranged assumption, and the screen is run over the range, not at a cherry-picked point.
  • Conservative choices (against the scenario, deliberately): the realized water-cycle response (~3.5 %/K, circulation-limited) is used, not the Clausius–Clapeyron ceiling (~7 %/K); the present-day measured (not larger reconstructed) ice volumes are the targets. If the screen passes under these conservative choices, it passes a fortiori.

1. The three sub-screens and their results

Sub-screen A — energy sufficiency

Can the ocean's heat reservoir supply, via latent heat of evaporation, the water to build an observed-scale ice sheet? (The evaporation latent heat is paid by the ocean cooling.)

Target (present-day measured) Ocean-cooling budget required Verdict
One continental ice sheet (Greenland, 2.85×10⁶ km³) cool top 1000 m by ~4.3 K, or top 400 m by ~10.8 K not a STOP
(same, shallow layer) cool top 100 m by ~43 K STOP (layer too shallow)
ALL present land ice (~30×10⁶ km³) in one pulse cool top 1000 m by ~46 K STOP (honest)

Reading A. A single continental ice sheet is funded by cooling the upper ~1 km of ocean by a few K — comfortably not a STOP. Building all present land ice in one pulse requires absurd cooling → honest STOP: the scenario must build ice by repeated and/or deeper thermal tapping, not one impulse. (The screen was not rigged to pass everything; it reports the STOP.)

Sub-screen B — accumulation rate (the scenario's distinctive "fast" claim)

At observed maritime snowfall rates, how long to build continental-thickness ice? Compared against nothing dated.

Observed snowfall (m w.e./yr) Ice growth (m/yr) Years to build Greenland-thickness (1670 m) Verdict
1.0 1.12 1,491 fast, not a STOP
2.0 2.24 746 fast, not a STOP
3.0 3.36 497 fast, not a STOP
4.0 4.48 373 fast, not a STOP

Reading B. At observed maritime rates, continental ice thickness accumulates in centuries to ~1.5 millennia → the "fast" claim is not forbidden. The screen makes no comparison to ~100-kyr orbital pacing (chronology, firewalled): it asks only whether the rate is physically attainable, and it is.

Sub-screen C — moisture enhancement (realized, not ceiling)

A warmer ocean raises evaporation. Using the realized ~3.5 %/K (not the 7 %/K ceiling): an ocean warmer by 3 / 5 / 8 / 10 K raises evaporation by +11 / +19 / +32 / +41 %. Consistent with, and reinforcing, sub-screen B. The lower (realized) value is used throughout.

C1's rate-capability is PHYSICALLY PERMITTED (Art. 4.3 mechanism/permission; Art. 5 UNLOCK). The "warm ocean under a cold sky builds continental ice fast" link does not require STOP-level parameters, under conservative (realized-efficiency, present-day-volume) assumptions. Occurrence of any specific glaciation remains [O] — the screen establishes permission, never history.

3. Open sub-gates (HOLD — genuinely unresolved, reported not hidden)

The screen is 0-dimensional (budgets and rates). Three physical questions it cannot resolve, kept visible per result-neutrality (Art. 3.3) and to prevent an E7-style overclaim (governance/11):

  1. (i) Spatial distribution. Observed high snowfall is coastal / narrow-band (lake/sea-effect is localized — the governance/07 caveat). Whether a warm global ocean produces continent-wide accumulation, vs. intense coastal bands with dry interiors, is not shown by a 0-D budget. HOLD.
  2. (ii) Accumulation vs. melt. Net growth needs the snow not to melt → cold continents (summers non-ablating). But a warm ocean also warms nearby land — a genuine physical tension. Resolution requires a cold, high-albedo atmosphere (the scenario's S1 dust loading), which is itself a scenario premise. HOLD.
  3. (iii) Conditionality on the warm ocean. The entire screen is run given a warm ocean. The warm ocean's origin (S1 jammed-water latent heat) and magnitude are [O] (Module 12 §1: S0 budget/releasability open). The screen proves permission conditional on S0/S1, not the warm ocean itself. HOLD.
