Module 36 — Petroleum weighted null: the ×2 enrichment retracted to ~1.0–1.3×

In plain terms. An earlier claim said the world's oil is about twice as concentrated toward rifted margins as chance would give. That number used the wrong baseline (it ignored that those margins also hold most of the sediment). Against a fair, sediment-weighted baseline the over-concentration shrinks to roughly 1.0–1.3×. The qualitative fact — oil does sit preferentially at rifted margins — survives; the inflated multiple does not. This corrects our own number, not the mechanism.

1. The bad null, fixed

The original ~2× compared the observed rifting-associated share against a flat null (every setting class equally likely). But petroleum needs sediment, and rifted margins hold a disproportionate share of sediment volume. The fair baseline is a volume-weighted null. Re-graded against it, the enrichment falls to ~1.0–1.3×.

2. What stands, what falls

Stands: the qualitative co-location of petroleum with rifting (the suite P1/P4). Falls: the “~2× strong enrichment” reading. The cascade's mechanism is untouched; one VP number is legitimately smaller.
