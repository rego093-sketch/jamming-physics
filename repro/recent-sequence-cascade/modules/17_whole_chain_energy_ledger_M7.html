Module 17 — Whole-Chain Energy Ledger (M7 capstone): the cascade closes under one parameter

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: 1 · Inherits: Modules 13 (C1), 14 (C2), 16 (single-relaxation theory), Master Scenario (M7 gate: "does the full chain close energetically without a STOP?").
Status: GATE module (the chain capstone). Computational; reproducible. Code: chain_energy_ledger.py (SEED=19; no RNG; no fitted parameter).
Standing grade: closure of physical permission under one parameter (Art. 4.3, Art. 5). Occurrence remains [O] (Art. 4.2).

What this module does, in one line. Modules 13–14 showed each climate hand-off is individually permitted. This module runs the whole-chain gate: does the entire cascade — relaxation heat → warm ocean → ice → loading → rupture → melt → equilibrium — close energetically under a single controlling parameter, with no physically absurd step, using measured constants only? It does, and the theory's distinctive build/melt asymmetry (Module 16 P3) falls out quantitatively.

0. Firewall (both directions)

Inputs are measured constants and present-day measured yardsticks (ocean area/volume, present ice masses, geothermal flux). No absolute chronology, no reconstructed ice volumes, no paleoclimate model, no fitted parameter. The one free quantity is the ocean heat anomaly (a warm-layer warming), run over a range. Any STOP is reported.

Reading. Across the range, one ocean-heat anomaly builds a fraction of a continental ice sheet (0.14–0.46× Greenland), loads the crust (feeding C2), and the melt is comfortably affordable — the chain closes with no STOP for a single ice sheet. (Building all present ice in one pulse remains the honest STOP from Module 13.) Everything scales with the one parameter — the theory's one-knob economy (Module 16), made quantitative.

2. The distinctive prediction, confirmed from measured constants (Module 16 P3)

Building ice goes through evaporation (the ocean pays the latent heat of vaporisation, ~2.45×10⁶ J/kg); melting it back pays only the latent heat of fusion (~3.34×10⁵ J/kg). From these measured constants alone:

Building ice is ~7.3× more expensive than melting it.

So the loop closes asymmetrically: a slow, ocean-heat-limited build, then a cheap, fast melt once the rupture restores warm circulation. Rapid termination is therefore a prediction of the theory, not a puzzle to be explained away — and it emerges with no fitted parameter, purely from two laboratory latent heats. This is exactly the kind of distinctive, falsifiable, theory-internal consequence the BUILD phase aims to produce (Module 16 §6).

3. The honest demanding link (flagged for the stress-test)

The source of the ocean heat is the one link the budget shows is demanding. If the heat comes from the gravitational energy of the reservoir release (water redistributing through a ~2 km drop), warming the ocean by 5–10 K requires releasing ~5–11% of the ocean's volume of water. The deep reservoir is ~1 ocean-equivalent (Module 12), so a few-percent release can supply it — large, but not absurd. Caveat held: gravitational potential energy is one source; mantle heat exposed by the rupture and the configurational energy of unjamming also contribute, so this is a sanity bound, not the only path. The flood-energy source is therefore plausible but the demanding link — flagged for the later stress-test, not claimed solved.

4. Verdict

The single-relaxation chain is energetically COHERENT under one parameter (Art. 5 UNLOCK at the whole-chain level): it builds ice, loads the crust, and melts back, with no STOP for a single ice sheet, using measured constants only and no chronology. The build/melt asymmetry (rapid termination) is a clean prediction. The flood-energy source is the demanding link, flagged. Occurrence remains [O].
