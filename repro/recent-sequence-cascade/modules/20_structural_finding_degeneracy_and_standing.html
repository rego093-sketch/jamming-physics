1. C-1 result: the deep-Earth substrate signature is DEGENERATE

The theory's engine is a deep reservoir of high-pressure "jammed" water (ice-VII) that can be released. Checking whether this differs from the mainstream:

Theory needs Mainstream already has Verdict
a deep water reservoir transition zone (410–660 km) holds ~0.6–1 ocean of water (hydrous ringwoodite/wadsleyite + free fluid/ice-VII) degenerate

Reading. The existence, the release, and the observable signature of deep water are all in the mainstream. The theory's deep-Earth claim is not distinctive in kind. Its only difference is, once again, rate / magnitude: a rapid, large, triggered, flood-producing release (theory) versus a slow, steady, small-degree (0.5–1% melt), convection-driven release (mainstream). And that difference is firewalled — absolute rate/magnitude is RECORD, both directions. C-1 yields no clean discriminator.

Every axis examined gives the same shape:

The pattern is structural, not accidental:

The theory's distinctive content is the rate / magnitude / timing (rapid, large, single coupled relaxation, recent-compressed). The project's own firewall (Constitution Art. 2–4) forbids absolute rate / timing as load-bearing — in both directions. The mainstream's distinctive content is the opposite rate / timing (slow, independent, deep-time). Therefore the theory and the mainstream are degenerate on precisely their core distinction — and that degeneracy is enforced by the project's own governance, not a flaw in the theory. What is left to distinguish them must be non-rate (a new present-tense observable or mechanism), and on every non-rate axis examined the theory has been found degenerate (the same observables, re-interpreted).

This is the deep reason the clean-discriminator hunt keeps returning "degenerate": the theory re-interprets the same present-tense observables under a different organizing principle (one fast relaxation vs many slow processes), and the only axis on which the principles truly diverge — rate/timing — is the one the firewall caps.

  1. It is not a defeat. The Constitution's stated deliverable (Art. 4.4) is "a physics-permitted, falsifiable, [O]-standing hypothesis with a graded evidence ledger — not a proof, ever." The theory has reached exactly that. Built, internally coherent, unified, parsimonious, falsifiable in principle, and honestly capped at [O]. This is the designed endpoint, not a shortfall.
  2. The theory's genuine standing: a unified, parsimonious alternative that buys flood + glaciation + opening + termination + salt/source-rock + the sawtooth from one substrate switch and one parameter, with no tuning, where the mainstream uses several independent, separately-tuned mechanisms. Its virtue is consilience-from-economy (an Occam-style reason to consider/prefer) — not a proof, and the cap stays [O].
  3. A clean empirical knockout is structurally hard under the firewall, because the distinctive axis (rate/timing) is firewalled and the non-rate axes are degenerate. The one genuinely remaining candidate is C-2 — a dimensionless energy/magnitude ratio that the one-parameter budget predicts and the mainstream does not (ratios sidestep both the size confound and the rate firewall). C-2 is hard and not yet established; it is left open, not declared impossible.
  4. The mainstream is not refuted, and is not weaponised. Its deep-water release, its rift sedimentology, its feedback-driven sawtooth are all robust; the theory offers a more economical reading of the same facts, held at [O].
