Module 34 — Deglacial-pattern discriminator: non-discriminating (a prior refutation retracted)

In plain terms. Old ice sheets left behind sets of nested ridges (moraines) as they shrank. You can read those ridges two ways — as a staircase of pauses during a steady retreat, or as the ice pushing back out again. Both fit what we see on the ground today, so the ridges alone cannot decide between the two pictures. An earlier version of this work claimed they refuted the clean melt; that claim leaned on dated ordering between hemispheres, which the firewall forbids — so it has been withdrawn.
