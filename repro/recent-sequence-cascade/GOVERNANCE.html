GOVERNANCE — the VP Recent-Sequence cascade constitution

This document fixes the rules the cascade is built under, so any reader can audit it against a stable standard rather than against taste. It is the cascade-level statement of the VP-SPEC discipline inherited from the foundation and the Atlantic-opening volume.

Article 1 — The question, and the occurrence cap

1.1 The cascade asks only whether a coupled Flood → Glaciation → Atlantic-opening relaxation, driven by one release of a jammed deep-water reservoir, is physically permitted by measured constants and the VP jamming engine.

1.2 — Occurrence cap. Any statement that such a sequence (or any of its faces) occurred in Earth history is capped at grade [O] (open). No chain of reasoning inside the cascade may raise a past-occurrence claim above [O]. Only mechanism and present-tense measurement may be graded higher.

1.3 Whether the sequence occurred is a separate, pre-registered question, decided by independent evidence (notably independent geochronology), not by the construction here.

Article 2 — The bidirectional chronology firewall

2.1 Absolute chronology (dates, ages, durations) is forbidden as a load-bearing premise in both directions: the cascade neither asserts "deep time" nor smuggles in "recent." Absolute timescales are held as record only.

2.2 The inherited geodynamics volume concedes the U–Pb continental break-up ages at full strength (it does not contest them by inheritance/reservoir/contamination arguments) and stays chronology-agnostic. The cascade adopts this posture without modification.

2.3 The load-bearing case is stated as "a rapid relaxation phase is physically permitted if the bounded boundary conditions obtain," compatible with either timescale — precisely so that the decision is carried by independent dating, not by any internal technicality (e.g. magnetic-stripe patterns, which are degenerate under uniform time rescaling).

Article 3 — Data grading (what may be load-bearing)

Observations are tiered by directness; only the top tiers may bear weight.

Tier Meaning Load-bearing?
D0 raw observation / direct measurement (drilled lithology, recovered phase, seismic geometry) yes (ACTIVE)
D1 one-step reduction (rate from thickness, modulus from a measured curve) yes (ACTIVE)
D2–D3 model-light inference from D0/D1 yes (ACTIVE), flagged
D4 model-dependent reconstruction (paleo-reconstruction, chronology-tuned series) record only
D5 absolute dates / orbital-tuned durations record only

A claim may be promoted to [F]/[V] only on D0–D3; D4–D5 inputs are carried as record and never decide a grade.

Article 4 — No-tuning, single substrate, build-then-grade

4.1 — No-tuning. No parameter is fitted to make a result come out. Every number is a measured input or a derived consequence. SEED = 19 convention; computations are deterministic and scripted.

4.2 — Single substrate. One engine — the R19 jammed ⇄ unjammed bistable switch — underlies every step (substrate S0, the rupture engine, the build/melt asymmetry are the same switch in different settings). The cascade does not introduce a new mechanism per phenomenon.

4.3 — Build-then-grade. The cascade's distinctive geometry is developed first; honest qualifications (openness, degeneracy, holds) are recorded at the end of each step, not used to retreat before the construction is built. A recorded failure mode is premature retreat to a default; the discipline is to build, then grade.

4.4 — Anti-contamination. At each step the cascade checks whether a claim is an unexamined default and strips it — without manufacturing a distinction where none exists. (The systematic comparison to alternative frameworks is the deferred stress-test, conducted separately.)

Article 5 — Verdict scheme

Grade Meaning
[F] forced / derived — follows from measured constants + the engine
[V] verified — measured directly, or reproduced by a SEED-fixed engine
[L] leaning — a well-anchored inference, not yet forced
[O] open — undecided; includes all past-occurrence claims (Art. 1.2)

Precedence [F] > [V] > [L] > [O]. FAIL/INCONCLUSIVE are contagious: nothing downstream may cite a failed or inconclusive result as support.

Article 6 — Reproducibility obligation

6.1 Every load-bearing computation is a runnable, SEED-fixed script; every load-bearing observation is a fetchable source. The mapping is given in REPRODUCIBILITY_MAP.md.

6.2 Inherited results are not taken on faith: the inherited engine bundle is re-run (39/39 PASS) and its integrity checked (60/60 SHA-OK) as part of this package, and every inherited quantity is checked against its source (Module 21).

6.3 — Falsification = discovery. An honest negative is a finding. Corrections (e.g. a retracted texture argument, a demoted prediction) are recorded openly in the module ledger, not silently removed.

These rules are fixed for the cascade. A reader who disagrees with a grade can locate the exact article it rests on and the exact script or source that backs it. That auditability — not rhetorical confidence — is the basis on which this package asks to be trusted.

Article 7 — Audit of interpretation and of data

This article is added after a live demonstration of its necessity during the development of this package's biology↔history bridge. It is binding on every component (cascade, DNA emergence, fossil audit) and on any future strand.

7.0 — The principle. Nothing is load-bearing because someone — an authority, a textbook, a prior model, or this assistant's own memory — says it. Two distinct things must be independently audited before either may be cited:

  1. Interpretation must be audited. Any explanatory model is held to its grade and no higher — including the mainstream one. An external interpretation may not be imported to settle the meaning of an observation. A favored interpretation and a disfavored interpretation of the same observation receive the same grade. Symmetry is mandatory: if a flood explanation of a pattern is [O], then a taphonomic or demographic explanation of the same pattern is also [O]. Stripping a disfavored model while quietly installing a favored one as if it were the data is a firewall violation, not an analysis.

  2. Data must be audited. A data claim is not accepted from assertion, summary, or recollection. It is recomputed from the primary source: fetch the database, count, and report the raw numbers. "The record is rich / sparse / absent" is an empirical claim that must be backed by an actual count over actual records, not by memory or by citation of a conclusion. Only the recomputed quantity is [V]; descriptions of its shape are [V] only insofar as they describe the recomputed numbers.

7.1 — The recorded case (so the rule is concrete, not abstract). In developing the fossil-count bridge, two errors were made and corrected in the open:

  • Data error. The Holocene record was asserted from memory to be "densely documented." This was wrong. The correction was to load a real global radiocarbon database (p3k14c, 179,689 dates) and count: early-Holocene human-remains dates are in fact single dozens per millennium. The original observation that ancient remains are sparse was correct, and the assertion-from-memory was withdrawn.
  • Interpretation error. The sparseness was then explained by importing an external model (taphonomic bias) as if it were the settled fact, while the flood interpretation was held [O]. This asymmetry was a violation. The correction was to demote every causal model — taphonomy, demography, flood — to [O], leaving the recomputed counts as the only [V] object, and to route all causal attribution to the history channel.

7.2 — The standing rule. Going forward, every claim in this package is checked on both axes before it is graded: is the interpretation being imported rather than earned? and is the datum recomputed from source rather than recalled? This auditing posture — applied to others' conclusions, to mainstream defaults, and to this assistant's own outputs alike — is itself part of the constitution. The willingness to recompute a number and to refuse a borrowed explanation, symmetrically and without omission, is the discipline on which the grades rest.

7.3 — No exemption for convenience. The rule binds in the direction that is inconvenient as well as the one that is welcome. An external model that supports a desired conclusion is held to [O] exactly as one that opposes it; a remembered "fact" that is flattering to the framework is recomputed exactly as one that is not. The firewall is two-edged by design (Art. 2); Article 7 is the procedure that keeps it so.

Article 7 records why the grades elsewhere in this package can be trusted: not only is each claim mapped to a script or a source, but every interpretation has been checked for being imported, and every datum has been checked for being recalled rather than recomputed. Where that check failed, it is logged here in the open.
