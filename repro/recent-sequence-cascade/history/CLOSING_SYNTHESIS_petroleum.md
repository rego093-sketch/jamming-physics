## 4. My contribution — two ideas to finish the strand

These are mine, offered to fill the remaining gaps rather than wait for them to be handed over.

### Idea 1 (a clincher candidate): the heavy-oil belt as an independent meltwater tracer
M42 implies something testable that no one set out to test: **the global distribution of
heavy/biodegraded oil independently maps where cold glacial meltwater reached shallow
reservoirs.** That is a SECOND fingerprint of the glaciation, fully independent of the
source-rock geography (M41). The build: overlay the world's heavy-oil/tar-sand provinces
(Athabasca, Orinoco, W Siberia heavy belts, Russian platform) on continental ice-sheet +
meltwater-routing reconstructions, and test the overlap. If heavy oil sits preferentially
in the meltwater-reach zones, the glaciation-as-driver thesis gains an independent leg — and
this is firewall-clean (present-tense oil quality + present-tense basin position). This is
the single most promising remaining build, and it is original to this analysis.

### Idea 2 (the right epistemic frame): coherence is the result, stop hunting one knockout
Given the firewall removes rate/timing — exactly where the one-parameter coupling diverges
most from mainstream — a single discriminating ratio (C-2) is structurally hard, and chasing
it risks overfitting. The honest, defensible strength of this program is the **8-fold
present-tense convergence** (§2). I'd recommend stating *that* as the headline claim — "one
coupled mechanism reproduces eight independent present-tense observables, parameter-free" —
rather than promising a knockout the firewall makes unlikely. That is both more honest and,
frankly, more impressive than a single contrived ratio.

### On C-2, concretely (so it isn't just deferred)
If one ratio is still wanted, the most principled candidate: **(salt-giant volume) / (source-
rock organic-carbon mass)** measured across several basins. One coupled event with one energy
budget predicts these co-vary at a roughly FIXED ratio (both scale with the same accommodation
× restriction); independent deep-time processes do not require any fixed ratio. It is testable
present-tense (both are measurable volumes/masses today). My prior: likely scattered (basins
differ in evaporation, clastic dilution), so probably weak/[O] — but it is the cleanest shot,
and worth one honest attempt before declaring the discriminator question closed.
