## 0. The one distinction everything hangs on

**"Thought" (생각/사고) is an umbrella, not a faculty. "Cognition" (인지) is one faculty under it.**

- *The sleeper test.* A sleeping person is not doing **cognition** (인지 = awake, goal-directed
  processing), yet mental activity continues — dreams, memory consolidation. So **cognition cannot be
  the umbrella**; it is one room in the house. The everyday word that names the *whole house* is
  **"thought / mental process."**
- Therefore: do **not** collapse everything to "cognition," and do **not** leave "thought" undefined.
  **Define "thought" as the union of the faculties in §1**, name each room, and close the house room by
  room. "Cognition" then sits inside as the *awake-processing* room — no more, no less.

## 2. Worked example — why "dream" proves the structure

A dream is the clean case the whole reframe turns on:

- It is **"thought"** in the loose sense (mental content, imagery, narrative).
- It is **not "cognition"** — the dreamer is asleep; awake goal-directed control is offline.
- Yet the package **already has the mechanism** (F4 consolidation + F7 sleep state + F8 mentation on
  that substrate).

So the dream is *not* an unhandled gap; it is a faculty whose only missing piece was the word pointing
at it. Three different things — waking cognition, dreaming, sensation — were each handled, and "thought"
was the one loose word stretched across all of them. That stretch was the confusion. The table fixes it.

## 3. The closing direction, stated so it cannot be philosophically misread

This is **science, not philosophy** — and it is built to be closed *without* importing a philosophical
claim. Two rules keep "thought is closed" from ever being heard as "consciousness is solved":

1. **The closure is on the MECHANISM axis only.** When the census passes, the claim is: *the listed
   faculties are mechanistically built, frozen, and reproducible.* Nothing more.
2. **The FELT axis (Axis A) is orthogonal and out of scope.** Whether any faculty is *experienced* —
   whether there is something it is like — is a **different axis** that the decomposition does not lie
   on. The closure makes **no claim** on it. `consciousness_claim = 0`, `hard_problem_open = 1` stand.

The mandated closure sentence (to appear wherever closure is asserted): *"Thought (defined as the §1
decomposition) is closed on the mechanism axis. Whether any of it is felt — Axis A — is a separate,
orthogonal question on which this closure asserts nothing."*

## 4. What is genuinely open is NOT "unclosed thought"

Two things remain open, and **neither is a piece of thought left undone** — each is a *different kind*
