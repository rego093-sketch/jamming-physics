Date: 2026-09-28. Four scripts that the cosmology pages cite had been lost. They are re-implemented here in the style of `../legacy_v2_3/`. Each script is deterministic, needs only numpy (matplotlib is optional, with `MPLBACKEND=Agg`), and runs in under 15 s. No parameter was adjusted to hit a page number. Nothing under `docs/` was edited.

## Honest summary

- **SNe:** the page's χ²/dof values reproduce exactly. The label "degenerate / fits equally well" does not. With equal parameters, Δχ² = +86.6 favours ΛCDM, and +195 once the errors are rescaled. This agrees with the finding in `legacy_v2_3/README.md`.
- **Hubble tension:** ηδ_loc ≈ 0.08 reproduces as ln(73.04/67.4), under the page's own non-predictive HYP/SPEC framing.
- **Bullet offset:** the page's 0.41 Mpc and the 0.2–0.6 Mpc band do not reproduce without an unstated path length. Every input is chosen by hand, and the calculation does not discriminate VP from dark matter.
- **Lensing consistency:** 4.4 and 0.2 reproduce. γ = 1 is an input, and the derived a₀ fits NGC 2403 worse than the empirical a₀.
