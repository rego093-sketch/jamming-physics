## Summary of mismatches and flags
1. **ch12**: the "10¹²⁰" ratio does not follow from the page's own inputs (they give 10¹²²). The Olbers resolution is degenerate in magnitude with FRW, and the energy closure of the absorber is not addressed.
2. **ch13**: the page result (0.250) is reproduced from textbook inputs. My own crude first-principles cross-check lands at 0.19. That shows the target is not trivially reproduced by a crude network. Precision codes give 0.247. The D/H step size is an unconstrained illustrative parameter; only the direction is robust.
3. **ch_jet_acceleration**: Γ→w₀ reproduced. "Matching what is seen" fails for GRB opening angles (observed 2–10° against 1/Γ ≤ 0.6°).
4. **ch_solar_activity**: the honest negative is reproduced as stated.
