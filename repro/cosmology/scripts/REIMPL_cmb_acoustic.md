# Re-implementation: CMB and acoustic-scale scripts (cosmology)

**Date.** 2026-09-28.
**What this is.** The pages cite seven scripts that are lost. These are new implementations written from what the pages say each script did. They are not the lost originals. They follow the legacy v2.3 style: a docstring with the claim, the inputs and their sources, the algorithm and the expected output. They use numpy only (matplotlib is optional, with `MPLBACKEND=Agg`) and are deterministic (`SEED = 19` wherever random numbers are drawn).
**Run.** `cd repro/cosmology/scripts && MPLBACKEND=Agg python3 <script>.py`. Each script's output is saved in `figs/<script>.out.txt` and its figures in `figs/`. The slowest script takes about 55 s.
**Rule followed.** No parameter was adjusted to hit a number on a page. Where a page number could not be reproduced, the table says so.

## Status summary

- **All seven scripts reproduce what the pages grade as open.** None of them derives 150 Mpc. The absolute 2.725 K remains anchored.
- **Page numbers not reproduced:**
  - cavity heights 0.91 and 0.17 (damping scale not recorded);
  - "10-decade gap" (computed 20.1);
  - 0.075c (computed 0.035c);
  - β̂ ≈ 0.501 (the companion's own code gives 0.464);
  - 3π² "within 1 %" (actually 3.7 %).
- **Reconstructed without a formula on the page:** the "cosmic-mean inflow length".
- **Circular by construction:** the Chapter 9 energy balance and floor value.
