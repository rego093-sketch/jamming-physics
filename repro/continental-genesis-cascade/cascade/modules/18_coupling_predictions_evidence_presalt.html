Module 18 — Evidence for the Coupling Predictions (P4 co-occurrence, P5 co-variation): the South Atlantic pre-salt, and a multi-pathway refinement

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: 1 · Inherits: Module 16 (the five distinctive predictions P1–P5), Module 15 (multi-dimensional / no-over-generalization discipline), Master Scenario.
Status: EVIDENCE module (Roadmap T1.1 + T1.2). Gathers and grades evidence for the theory's coupling predictions. BUILD-then-grade: the realised co-occurrence is presented first; the honest grading (degeneracy, a counterexample-turned-refinement, the still-distinctive part) follows.
Standing grade: occurrence/age remain [O] (Art. 4). Firewall held both directions (the orbital/age numbers are RECORD).

What this module does, in one line. Module 16 predicted that, because one parameter controls everything, the relaxation's products should co-occur (P4) and co-vary in magnitude (P5). The South Atlantic pre-salt — at the very rift where the Atlantic opened — is a spectacular realised case of the co-occurrence; graded multi-dimensionally, it is real but degenerate, it is the arid (not glacial) flavour of the relaxation (a refinement, per the no-over-generalization rule), and it leaves the co-variation as the still-distinctive, still-open test.

1. The realised co-occurrence (BUILD: the theory predicts it; here it is)

At the conjugate South Atlantic margins (Brazil: Santos, Campos, Espírito Santo; West Africa: Lower Congo, Kwanza, Gabon), three of the relaxation's products co-occur in one stratigraphic package at the rift that opened the Atlantic:

Product Observable proc_grade status
Rifting / opening syn-rift horst-and-graben basement; extensional faulting continuing up to the base of the salt; this is the South Atlantic opening rift D0/D1 (seismic basement structure) ACTIVE
Organic source rock pre-salt/sag organic-rich lacustrine mudstones + carbonates ("super-rich, thickly developed" — e.g. Bucomazi, Guaratiba sag) — the source rocks of a giant petroleum province D0/D1 (drilled lithology + geochemistry) ACTIVE
Evaporite (salt) an Aptian salt giant up to ~2.5 km thick capping the sag sequence D0/D1 (drilled/seismic thickness) ACTIVE
Rate observable salt deposition up to several cm/yr (a fast deposition rate, present-tense-style observable) D1 (rate from thickness; period attribution excluded) ACTIVE
Volcanic association pre-salt volcanics (basalt flows/tuffs), near the Paraná–Etendeka flood-basalt province D1 ACTIVE

Reading. The theory's predicted suite — rift + organic source rock + salt (+ volcanics) — is observed together, at the Atlantic opening itself, drilled and seismically imaged. As a realised instance of P4 (material co-occurrence) and of the broader "flood/opening + petroleum/salt residue" coupling, this is strong and concrete.

2. Honest grading — three independent dimensions (the multi-dimensional view, per Module 15)

The author's standing rule (do not over-generalise; look at every axis) applied to this evidence:

Axis A — does it distinguish the theory? → DEGENERATE

Mainstream rift-basin sedimentology also predicts this suite: rifting makes restricted basins → climate-induced lake-level drawdown → evaporites; restricted anoxic rift lakes preserve organics. Indeed the literature explains the salt by exactly that — "a climate-induced lake-level drawdown generated the shallow-water environments for evaporite precipitation," extension continuing to the base of the salt. So the co-occurrence is observably real but does not separate the single-relaxation theory from mainstream rift sedimentology. Honest verdict: consistent with the theory, supportive of the coupling picture, but not distinctive.

Axis B — temperature flavour → ARID, not glacial (a refinement, not a defeat)

The South Atlantic salt formed in a warm, arid Aptian setting (evaporative drawdown), not a glacial-meltwater setting. For this instance the evaporite pathway is arid-evaporative, not glacial. This is a counterexample to the glaciation coupling for this case — and, handled per Module 15, it becomes a refinement:

The single relaxation can produce its evaporite/source-rock residue by more than one pathway — arid drawdown (warm rift basins: South Atlantic pre-salt) or cold/glacial restriction (high-latitude basins: Bazhenov, with cold-water indicators, Module 15). The "one engine" has multiple regional expressions set by local temperature/setting — exactly the latitude-dependent, non-over-generalised picture the author has insisted on, and consistent with the open transport origin {glacial, flood, arid, other}.

So the South Atlantic is the arid-drawdown flavour; it supports co-occurrence and rapidity but is not evidence for the glacial coupling — and saying so is the honest, multi-axis reading.

Axis C — rate → fast observable (supports rapidity; period firewalled)

Salt deposition at several cm/yr and a >2 km salt giant are fast by any measure — a present-tense-style rate observable that supports the rapidity dimension. Held out (RECORD, both directions): the cyclostratigraphic period/duration attributions (e.g. an orbital ~22-kyr-cycle reading, a ~573-kyr total) are D4/D5 chronology — named, not load-bearing, in either direction. The observable (thickness, cm/yr rate) stays; the clock does not.

3. The still-distinctive, still-open test: P5 magnitude co-variation

P4 co-occurrence is degenerate (Axis A). The distinctive prediction is P5: because one parameter sets all magnitudes, the relaxation's products should co-vary — bigger extension ⇒ bigger salt ⇒ richer source rock — whereas mainstream treats these as separately-controlled processes and predicts no tight co-variation.

  • Suggestive present-tense evidence: the South Atlantic salt giant tracks the central, maximum-extension segment of the opening rift, and asymmetric rifting produced asymmetric salt/oil distributions on the two conjugate margins (Brazil vs West Africa). This hints at extension-magnitude controlling salt/source-rock magnitude — the direction P5 predicts.
  • But it is not yet a clean test. A firewall-clean P5 test requires, across multiple rifted/flood systems, measured present-tense magnitudes — extension/opening width, salt volume, source-rock richness/volume — and a check of whether they co-vary (theory) or are independent (mainstream). That dataset is specified, not yet assembled.

This is the genuine next experiment (Roadmap T1.1, sharpened): a magnitude-co-variation test across rift/flood systems, present-tense geometry only, ages held out.

4. Honest standing after this evidence

  1. P4 (co-occurrence): observably real and concrete (South Atlantic pre-salt = rift + salt + source rock, at the Atlantic opening) — but degenerate; supportive, not distinctive.
  2. Multi-pathway refinement: the relaxation makes evaporites/source rocks by arid drawdown (warm) or cold/glacial restriction — local temperature sets the flavour. The South Atlantic is the arid flavour (a refinement gained by honest multi-axis reading, not a defeat).
  3. Rate: salt at cm/yr + a 2 km salt giant is a fast observable (supports rapidity); the orbital/duration numbers are firewalled out, both directions.
  4. P5 (co-variation): the distinctive prediction, with suggestive support (salt giant ∝ maximum extension; asymmetric rift ⇒ asymmetric salt/oil) and a clean test specified but unrun.
  5. Occurrence/age remain [O]; the inverse contaminant ("salt + rift ⇒ recent") is not asserted.
