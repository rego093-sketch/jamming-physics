Module 27 — Petroleum metastability (I): the oil-cracking clock, and the honest charge-vs-preservation gap

One line. Light, hydrogen-rich oil is a loaded spring: at the measured present-day reservoir temperature it thermally cracks to gas + pyrobitumen with a forward lifetime τ(T) that is computed from laboratory kinetics and uses no absolute age. τ collapses steeply with temperature — above roughly 180–190 °C every plausible kinetic choice gives τ far shorter than any deep-time claim, so abundant light oil there puts a heavy preservation burden on the deep-time reading. But this is honest both ways: a standalone cracking clock constrains residence since generation, not basin age, and the mainstream escapes it by continuous/recent charge from an old source. The clock therefore becomes a true discriminator only when paired with present-tense evidence that local generation is not ongoing (an over-mature source) and that the accessible volume cannot be resupplied — that pairing is the next task. Occurrence stays [O], both directions; only τ(T) is load-bearing.

1. Thesis and firewall (present-tense, not chronology)

The cascade's signature object is a metastable loaded state that relaxes. Light oil is exactly such a state in the petroleum system: a high-hydrogen-to-carbon (high H/C), low-entropy hydrocarbon mixture that is thermodynamically metastable relative to its products (methane + heavier residue/pyrobitumen). It does not introduce a new mechanism — it is the same loaded-spring/relaxation engine, realised in organic matter.

The relaxation rate is the first-order thermal-cracking rate at the reservoir temperature, an Arrhenius quantity:

k(T) = A · exp(−Eₐ / (R·T)) , τ(T) = 1 / k(T)

  • T is the present-day measured reservoir temperature — a present-tense observable (drilled, logged).
  • A, Eₐ are laboratory-measured kinetics — measured inputs, not fitted (Art. 4.1).
  • τ(T) is a forward e-folding lifetime — the analogue of a half-life. It is a property of the system as it is now; it neither uses nor asserts any absolute age.

Firewall compliance (Art. 2, symmetric). τ(T) is present-tense and is the only load-bearing output. The fraction of light oil surviving a residence time is reported only against the dimensionless ratio r = residence/τ, never against an absolute time, so no chronology enters in either direction. The statement "this oil is young" and the statement "this oil is old" are both forbidden; what is graded is the present-tense metastability τ and the burden each reading must then carry. This is the same firewall-neutral posture already used for the radiocarbon contamination arithmetic (Module 26 A).

2. The clock (repro/oil_cracking_clock.py)

Representative single first-order oil→gas kinetics (LOCK: Eₐ = 230 kJ/mol, A = 1×10¹³ /s). Real cracking is a distributed-Eₐ process (Pepper & Dodd 1995; Tsuzuki et al. 1999; Behar et al. 2008); this transparent single reaction is calibrated to the empirical oil "deadline" (~150–160 °C over tens of Myr) and is meant as a reproducible screen, not a replacement for compositional kinetics.

Measured reservoir T forward lifetime τ
90 °C ~3,800 Gyr (effectively eternal)
110 °C ~72 Gyr
130 °C ~2 Gyr
150 °C ~78 Myr
160 °C ~17 Myr
170 °C ~4 Myr
180 °C ~1 Myr
190 °C ~0.3 Myr

Two facts matter, and one caveat.

(a) The curve is exponentially steep. Below ~130 °C oil is stable for far longer than any geological time — cool reservoirs preserve oil with no difficulty, and the deep-time reading carries no burden there. The metastability argument has no purchase on cool oil and must not pretend otherwise.

(b) There is a robust high-temperature burden zone. Above ~180–190 °C, τ is at most a few million years for every plausible kinetic pair (sensitivity run: at 190 °C, τ ranges only ~0.02–5 Myr across Eₐ = 220–250 kJ/mol). So abundant light oil found at such temperatures is metastable on ≤ Myr scales regardless of kinetic choice — and the deep-time reading must then explain survival over hundreds of Myr. That burden, in the hot zone, is forced [F] and kinetics-robust.

Caveat (honest, Art. 7). Between ~130 and ~180 °C the result is exquisitely sensitive to Eₐ: a 10 kJ/mol shift moves τ by more than ten-fold (at 150 °C, τ spans ~5 Myr to ~2 Gyr across the literature range). So the quantitative burden in the mid-temperature band is not robust; only the hot-zone statement is. This sensitivity is recorded, not hidden.

3. The honest plausibility gap — what a standalone cracking clock cannot do

This is the physical weakness the strand must own. The cracking clock constrains the oil's residence since it was generated/emplaced — not the age of the basin or the formation. The two are different quantities, and the mainstream routinely separates them:

In the standard petroleum-system picture, generation is continuous. An old, slowly-maturing source rock keeps expelling fresh oil over geological time; a reservoir can hold recently generated light oil even inside a very old basin. So "hot light oil exists now" is read as "it was charged recently," and the cracking clock is satisfied trivially — the oil is young (short residence) even on the deep-time account, because the rock kept making more.

Therefore, by itself, the cracking clock is degenerate (Module 20's pattern again): both readings satisfy it. The clock measures a real present-tense lifetime [V], but it does not discriminate deep-time from recent — the mainstream escapes through continuous/recent charge. Grading it as a discriminator: [O].

The physical cause of the gap, stated precisely. The escape exists because the source is treated as an inexhaustible, still-active generator. The clock only bites if that escape is physically closed — i.e., if one can show, present-tense, that the local source can no longer be generating the observed oil, or that the observed accumulation cannot be continuously resupplied. Two such closures exist, both measurable and both date-free:

  1. Over-mature local source. Source maturity is a present-tense measurement (e.g. vitrinite-reflectance equivalent, biomarker maturity ratios, residual generative potential). If the local source kitchen is past the oil-generation stage (spent), it cannot be currently charging light oil — yet light oil is present and uncracked at temperature. Continuous generation is then physically excluded for that system, and the cracking burden returns in full. The remaining mainstream escape narrows to long-distance recent migration, which carries its own (testable) timing and mass constraints.

  2. Volume / mass balance. The total mass of light oil + gas + residue in the accessible accumulation, compared against the source's present residual generative capacity, is a present-tense ratio (no rate, no date). If the accumulated mass exceeds what the present residual source could supply, "trickle re-charge" cannot account for it; a large, single, recent charge is required — which is the cascade's reading, and which the slow-continuous picture cannot give.

Neither closure is a knockout on its own (migration and re-charge histories can be invoked), but each removes one escape, and the convergence — light + hot (short τ) + over-mature local source (no current generation) + large accessible volume (no trickle re-charge) — is what makes the deep-time preservation burden genuinely heavy. This is the same consilience-from-economy logic the rest of the cascade rests on, now applied to one accumulation.

Honest status of the strand after this module: the cracking physics is real and the hot-zone burden is forced, but the strand is not yet a discriminator. It becomes one only with §3.1–3.2 attached. That is the next petroleum task, and it is what "the scenario is under-developed" precisely means here.

4. Why this still matters, and why it is worth pursuing hard

  1. It is the one axis that can break the degeneracy. Module 20 found every other axis degenerate with the mainstream (rate/timing firewalled; non-rate axes re-interpretable). Petroleum metastability is different because the mainstream itself carries a real, acknowledged tension here (the preservation problem of light oil at temperature; oil found where deep-time kinetics predict gas). If §3's closures hold for real systems, the deep-time reading carries a quantified burden that no free re-interpretation removes — a discriminating result the other axes cannot deliver. This is why it is a candidate maximum-strength support, not a weak point, and why it should not be conceded cheaply.
  2. It frees the strand from the unfavourable texture result. The basal-texture test (Module 15) asked how the source rock was deposited — a past process, and it leaned unfavourable. The metastability clock asks about the present state of the accumulated oil, which is independent of source-rock depositional texture. The Module 15 result therefore does not touch this argument.
  3. Single-engine economy. No new mechanism: light oil is a loaded spring (metastable high-H/C state) and cracking/leakage is its relaxation — the cascade's universal switch, realised in organic matter.
