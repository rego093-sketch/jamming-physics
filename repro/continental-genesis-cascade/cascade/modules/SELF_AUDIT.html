Self-audit — firewall violations found and removed

In plain terms. When the assistant first built part of this strand it claimed it had refuted some strong readings. On review, two of those “refutations” weren't earned by present-day data — they quietly leaned on dated orderings (which the rules forbid) and on a strawman. Method

For every load-bearing claim the question was: is this a present-tense datum, or an imported interpretation (especially a mainstream one) wearing a data costume? A present-tense fact is load-bearing [V]. An absolute date, a rate, a temporal ordering, or a “this is what it means” reading is held out by the firewall ([O]/RECORD).

What changes after the correction

The corrected tally: zero clean present-tense refutations of the framework (the two claimed were artifacts of imported interpretation); four non-discriminating results (M34, M35, M37, M39); one statistical retraction of a VP number (M36, mechanism untouched); one [O] both ways (M38, with honest two-clock reasoning); and one genuine new structural object (the M33 fork plus the observed high-latitude bistable lock). The chain returns to its honest standing — degenerate with mainstream on present-tense axes, [O]-capped on rate/timing, neither confirmed nor refuted. The lesson

The firewall is hardest to keep exactly when importing “mainstream evidence,” because that evidence arrives chronologically framed. Strip every dataset to its present-tense core before it touches the load-bearing column; the ages and orderings stay RECORD — for and against. The framework does not deny the moraines, the laminae, or the oil; it claims a deeper mechanism, and the present-tense data — read without smuggled interpretation — neither refutes nor confirms that mechanism.

Self-audit complete. Falsification = discovery — applied to the auditor's own work. The corrections are recorded mark-never-erase; the strand's modules carry each one inline.
