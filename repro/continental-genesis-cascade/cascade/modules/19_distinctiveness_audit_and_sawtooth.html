Module 19 — Distinctiveness Audit (anti-contamination): which predictions actually separate the theory from the mainstream, and the sawtooth as a parsimony test

What this module does, in one line. It asks of each of the theory's five predictions: does the mainstream predict this too? If yes → degenerate (cannot be load-bearing for distinctiveness). The audit finds that the product co-occurrence predictions are degenerate, the rate/structure predictions are the theory's real edge — and even the cleanest of those (the sawtooth) is a parsimony advantage, not a clean knockout. The honest result: the theory's case is consilience-from-economy, and a clean discriminator is still missing — which becomes the named next challenge.

The honest finding of the audit. The theory's product co-occurrence predictions (P1-rift-part, P4) are degenerate — the mainstream's ordinary rift processes predict them, so they cannot be load-bearing for the theory's distinctiveness, and leaning on them would be exactly the contamination the directive warns against. The theory's real edge is on the rate / structure / parsimony axis (P3/sawtooth, P5-co-variation, P2-single-pulse) — but each of those is either parsimony-advantaged-not-decisive (P3) or confounded/chronology-entangled (P2, P5).

2. The sawtooth as a parsimony test (the cleanest current case, graded honestly)

The observation (robust, both-sides agreed)

The late-Pleistocene ~100-kyr glacial cycles are strongly time-asymmetric: slow build (inception), fast termination (deglaciation) — the "sawtooth." This is well established in the proxy record. (Grade: the relative asymmetry of the record shape is D1 ACTIVE; the absolute durations — ~100 kyr, etc. — are D5 RECORD, firewalled both directions.)

What each side says

  • Single-relaxation theory: the asymmetry is forced by two laboratory latent heats — building ice pays the (large) latent heat of vaporisation (ocean-heat-limited → slow); melting pays only the (small) latent heat of fusion, aided by restored warm circulation (→ fast). One measured-constant principle, no tuning (Module 17, SH-16).
  • Mainstream: the asymmetry arises from nonlinear feedbacks — ice-albedo, CO₂ draw-down/ventilation, ocean stratification thresholds — tuned within the orbital-pacing framework. The mainstream also reproduces the sawtooth, but with acknowledged difficulties: the "100-kyr paradox" (orbital forcing has little ~100-kyr power), the mid-Pleistocene 41→100-kyr transition with no orbital change, and a "causality problem" (a termination appearing to precede its insolation trigger).

Honest verdict

The sawtooth is NOT a clean discriminator — both frameworks predict it. The theory's advantage is parsimony: it gets the asymmetry from one principle where the mainstream needs several tuned feedbacks that still leave open paradoxes. That is a legitimate scientific advantage (Occam-style), but it is not a falsification-level knockout, and it must not be overstated: the orbital framework remains robust overall; its difficulties are about the details of the 100-kyr cycle, not a collapse. Firewall caution: the theory's prediction is the relative asymmetry (slow build < fast melt) — which is firewall-clean (no absolute dates) — not the absolute 100-kyr period (which is chronology, RECORD).

3. Honest standing after the audit

  1. No clean knockout discriminator exists yet. Every prediction is either degenerate (co-occurrence — mainstream predicts it), parsimony-advantaged-but-not-decisive (sawtooth), or confounded / chronology-entangled (co-variation, single-pulse).
  2. The theory's real case is consilience-from-economy. It buys flood + glaciation + opening + termination + salt/source-rock + the sawtooth from one substrate switch and one parameter, with no tuning, where the mainstream uses several independent, separately-tuned mechanisms. That is a genuine theoretical virtue (parsimony + unification) — but parsimony is an argument for preference, not a proof, and the cap stays [O].
  3. The directive is satisfied: the theory is not leaning on contaminated (degenerate) support; its distinctiveness claim is now restricted to where it is real (parsimony on rate/structure), and that claim is stated at its true, modest strength.

4. The genuine open challenge (named, not papered over): find a CLEAN discriminator

The honest next target is a prediction where the theory and the mainstream diverge in a firewall-clean, present-tense observable — something the mainstream cannot also produce. Candidates to develop:

  • C-1 — a deep-Earth substrate signature. The theory's engine is jammed State-4 water (ice-VII). Does it predict a specific, measurable present-day deep-water distribution or phase-boundary signature that differs from mainstream mantle-water models? (A physics prediction, potentially clean — no chronology.)
  • C-2 — an energy-partitioning ratio. One budget predicts fixed ratios among flood / ice / opening energies. If any such ratio is observable in present-tense magnitudes, a match would be distinctive (the mainstream predicts no such fixed ratio). (Sharper version of P5, designed to beat the size confound by using ratios, not absolute sizes.)
  • C-3 — the single-pulse vs many-independent-events structure, expressed in a chronology-free observable (e.g. a structural/relational signature of one coupled relaxation vs many unrelated episodes) — the part of P2 that can be tested without the firewalled period.

A clean discriminator on any of these would move the theory from "parsimonious alternative held at [O]" toward a genuinely testable distinction. Until then, the theory stands as a unified, parsimonious, [O]-capped hypothesis — which is exactly what the constitution says it should be (Art. 4.4).

5. Re-pointed roadmap (anti-contamination)

  • Drop leaning on degenerate co-occurrence (P1-rift-part, P4) as evidence of distinctiveness — keep them only as consistency checks, clearly labelled.
  • Concentrate the distinctiveness case on rate / structure / parsimony (P3/sawtooth as parsimony; the energy closure as unification).
  • Hunt for a clean discriminator (C-1, C-2, C-3) — the real next experiments.
  • Hold the parsimony argument at its true strength: a reason to prefer, not a proof; occurrence stays [O].
