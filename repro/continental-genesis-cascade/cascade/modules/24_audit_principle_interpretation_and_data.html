Module 24 — The audit principle: check the interpretation and the datum

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: constitutional (narrative of GOVERNANCE Art. 7) · Inherits: Modules 22–23.
Status: STANDING RULE, with a recorded live case.
Standing grade: procedural — governs how every other grade is earned.

One line. Nothing is load-bearing because someone says it — not an authority, not a textbook, not a prior model, not this assistant's own memory. Before anything is cited, two independent audits must pass: the interpretation must be earned rather than imported (and held to its grade, symmetrically with its rivals), and the datum must be recomputed from the primary source rather than recalled.

1. Two axes, both mandatory

Interpretation audit. An explanatory model — including the mainstream one — is held to its grade and no higher. No external interpretation may be imported to settle the meaning of an observation. A favored and a disfavored interpretation of the same observation get the same grade. If a flood explanation of a pattern is [O], a taphonomic or demographic explanation of the same pattern is also [O]. Removing a disfavored model while quietly installing a favored one as if it were the data is a firewall violation.

Data audit. A data claim is not accepted from assertion, summary, or memory. It is recomputed from the primary source — fetch the records, count, report raw numbers. "Rich / sparse / absent" must be backed by an actual count over actual records. Only the recomputed quantity is [V].

2. The recorded case (this conversation, logged so the rule is concrete)

  • Data error → corrected. The Holocene record was asserted from memory as "densely documented." Wrong. Loading a real database (179,689 radiocarbon dates) and counting showed early-Holocene human remains are single dozens per millennium. The sparseness observation was correct; the assertion-from-memory was withdrawn.
  • Interpretation error → corrected. The sparseness was then explained by importing taphonomic bias as if settled, while the flood explanation was held [O] — an asymmetry. Corrected by demoting every causal model (taphonomy, demography, flood) to [O], leaving the recomputed counts as the only [V] object.

3. No exemption for convenience

The rule binds the inconvenient direction as well as the welcome one. A model that supports a desired conclusion is held to [O] exactly as one that opposes it; a remembered "fact" that flatters the framework is recomputed exactly as one that does not. The firewall is two-edged (Art. 2); this audit is the procedure that keeps it so — applied to others' conclusions, to mainstream defaults, and to this assistant's own outputs alike, without omission.

4. Standing

This posture is itself part of the constitution (GOVERNANCE Art. 7). The grades elsewhere can be trusted because each claim was checked on both axes: is the interpretation imported rather than earned? and is the datum recomputed rather than recalled? Where a check failed, it is logged in the open — here and in Module 23.

Grade: procedural; this is the rule by which [F]/[V]/[L]/[O] are assigned honestly.
