Module 29 — Whole-chain consolidation and the honest weak-link report

Author: Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
Tier: 1 (Recent-Sequence applications branch) · Inherits: the full Blueprint ledger, Modules 12–28, Constitution Art. 2/4/7. Consumes: the mechanical consistency audit repro/chain_consistency_audit.py.

One line. Mechanically auditing the whole cascade as a dependency graph gives three clean results. (1) The scaffolding has no circular reasoning — it is acyclic. (2) Six claims are unconditionally solid (they rest on nothing open): the high-pressure-water substrate, the gateway-gated flood mechanism, the measured snowfall rate, the engine at laboratory scale, the present-tense oil-cracking metastability, and the forced source-rock richness requirement. (3) Everything else is conditional, and the conditions converge on a small set of open premises — overwhelmingly the reservoir budget / ocean-heat source, then the glaciation premises (net accumulation, moisture reach, the cold-sky), then the rupture-scale premises (near-criticality, continental scaling). These stay [O]; they are not forced, and forcing them would invite the attack the author rightly wants to avoid. Two internal seams (the doubly-specified H-A trigger; the glaciation-vs-post-opening ordering) are the only places the internal causality is not yet consistent, and they must be closed by reasoning. The petroleum strand — the source-rock organic budget — is the strongest distinctive card, framed as economy, not proof.

1. The audit (repro/chain_consistency_audit.py)

The cascade is encoded as a graph of claims, each with its grade and what it rests on; the script checks acyclicity, separates unconditional from conditional load-bearing claims, and ranks the open premises by how many load-bearing claims depend on them. Result:

The scaffolding is acyclic — there is no circular reasoning. This is the first thing internal consistency requires, and it holds.

1.1 The unconditional solid backbone (state these with confidence)

These six rest on nothing open; they are the genuinely strong, defensible core:

  • High-pressure water / ice-VII exists (observed in natural diamonds).
  • A gateway-gated catastrophic flood is physically permitted, with the Nile incision + salt + gateway signature observed and drilled.
  • The maritime snowfall rate is measured (the input that forces the gap floor).
  • The void-suction / unjamming engine reproduces at laboratory scale (39/39).
  • Light oil is present-tense metastable — its forward cracking lifetime is a measured, date-free quantity (Module 27).
  • Source-rock richness forces three coincident conditions — the mass balance is forced (Module 28).

1.2 The open premises the whole chain converges on (leave these [O])

Ranked by how many load-bearing claims rest on each:

Open premise load-bearing claims resting on it leave [O] because
reservoir budget / ocean-heat source the most (the dominant root) sufficiency is unobserved; pinning it imports a magnitude → forcing it invites attack
net accumulation under a warm ocean (the cold-sky premise) the glaciation half the warm ocean is both fuel and melt-threat; the cold-sky is asserted, not screened
moisture reaches the continent interior the glaciation half a budget screen can't resolve the spatial pattern

continental-scale extrapolation of the engine the rupture half the engine reproduces at lab scale; the continental claim is a standing HOLD

Every strong-looking link in the chain (the fast ice build, the gap floor, the glaciation, the C2 trigger, the opening, the termination, even the petroleum economy) is conditional on this set. That is not a flaw to hide; it is the honest shape, and the discipline is to carry these as [O] and let independent work — or nothing — settle them.

2. The two internal seams (the only places internal causality is not yet consistent)

These are not evidence gaps; they are places the chain currently says two things. They must be closed for the internal causality to be non-contradictory (the author's hard requirement), and both close by reasoning, with no new data.

  1. The H-A trigger is doubly specified. One construction has the reservoir release collapse the enclosure and open the rupture immediately; the C2/gap construction has the delayed glacial load trigger it after the floor. Recommended closure: the release primes a near-critical rift (it does not open it), the glacial load is the trigger, and the engine is what drives the opening fast — which preserves the single-switch economy, makes the gap necessary, and is supported by the observed analogue (deglaciation faulting). This promotes near-criticality to load-bearing (its honest cost).
  2. The ordering seam. The cascade's glaciation is pre-rupture loading; the inherited volume's responses are post-rupture. These are sequential phases of one relaxation, not rival claims. Closure: state the single timeline explicitly and check that no inherited post-opening response requires an ice-free pre-state; if one does, revise the pre-loading magnitude (do not ignore the conflict).

Until these are written, the chain has a real internal inconsistency. They are the first thing to fix.

3. The weak-link report (ranked; honest strengths)

Tier 1 — internal-consistency gaps (fix first, by reasoning).
The two seams of §2. A logically inconsistent chain is worthless regardless of evidence, so these outrank every data task.

Tier 2 — the load-bearing open premises (leave [O]; name them so attacks are expected, not ambushes).
The reservoir budget / ocean-heat root above all, then the glaciation premises (net accumulation, moisture reach, cold-sky), then the rupture-scale premises (near-criticality, continental scaling). The correct posture is not to prove these but to state plainly that the chain is permitted if they hold, and that they are open both ways. This is the author's "leave it [O]" discipline applied exactly.

Tier 3 — petroleum discriminators not yet closed (the strongest card, still parsimony).
The source-rock economy (one event supplies supply + self-anoxia + low dilution) is the strongest distinctive claim, but it is [L] — a reason to prefer, not a proof. The cracking clock and the richness budget are real present-tense facts, but as deep-time-vs-recent discriminators they are [O] until paired with the closures (over-mature source, volume/mass-balance, the near-global synchrony of the world's giant source rocks). The present-phenomenon bar the author set — explaining where petroleum is found — routes here: petroleum location co-locating with the cascade signature (rift + salt + rich source) is [L] suggestive, and is the right next test.

Tier 4 — peripheral weak items (state weakly; do NOT lean on them, do NOT let them be the battlefield).
The basal-texture test (leans unfavourable — demoted from "the decisive test"); the elevation discriminator (a real test that failed/reversed, recorded); the mid-temperature cracking sensitivity (kinetics-dependent); and the over-scoped "rich source rock requires a catastrophe" (too strong → [O]). These are correctly weak, and the discipline is to describe them weakly and move on — being attacked on these would be the avoidable, unimportant fight the author warns against.

4. What this consolidation establishes

The cascade is internally acyclic, with a small, solid, unconditional backbone, a named set of open load-bearing premises carried honestly as [O], two internal seams that must be closed by reasoning, and one strongest distinctive card (source-rock economy) held at the honest grade of parsimony. The bar is the author's: explain present phenomena — above all the location of petroleum and its associated rift/salt/rich-source suite — more economically than the competing account, while never forcing an [O] above its grade. On that bar the cascade is a live, honestly-graded, internally-consistent-once-the-seams-close hypothesis — exactly the object the constitution prescribes, and nothing overclaimed.
