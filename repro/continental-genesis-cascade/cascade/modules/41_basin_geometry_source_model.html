Module 41 — 3D basin-geometry source model (the keystone): where the oil actually is

In plain terms. Why is oil where it is? Not simply by latitude. Thick ice piles on continents; when it melts fast, the meltwater and ground-up rock flush off the land into the next-door restricted basin — exactly where source rocks form. So the real control is how restricted the basin is, fed either by cold meltwater (high latitude) or warm seas (low latitude). The open ocean gets neither and stays barren. That is the actual map of the world's oil — North Sea, West Siberia, Persian Gulf, the Atlantic rifts — and the empty deep ocean.

One line. In 3D — ice-on-continents + basin restriction — the master variable is basin restriction, not latitude. Restricted/rift basins fed by meltwater (high-latitude route) or warm restricted seas (low-latitude route) are favourable; the open ocean is barren (well-mixed, oxygenated, no lid). The favourability has a high-latitude peak (continental ice melts → meltwater lid + glacial-flour nutrients) and a low-latitude shoulder, with a dip at the arid 30°. This MATCHES the real oil map (North Sea, W Siberia, Persian Gulf, Atlantic rifts) and the deep-ocean void.

1. Why 3D (the fix to the flat M40)

M40 was latitude-only and wrongly peaked at the equatorial open ocean. Ice piles thick on continents (land, mid-high latitude); melting fast, the meltwater + glacial flour flushes off the continent into the adjacent margin / restricted basin — exactly where source rocks form. So the axis is basin geometry (restriction) + meltwater routing, not zonal climate.

2. Two routes to an anoxic, productive basin (both gated by restriction)

(A) High-latitude meltwater route: thick continental ice melts → freshwater lid + glacial-flour nutrients flush into the down-slope basin → stratified, fertilised. (B) Low-latitude warm route: warm event ocean + tropical productivity in a restricted/evaporitic basin → stratified, fertilised. Open ocean gets neither restriction nor a lid → well-mixed, oxygenated → barren.

3. It matches the real map

The favourability field peaks in restricted basins downstream of continental ice and in warm restricted seas, and is barren in the open ocean — exactly the observed distribution (North Sea, West Siberia, Persian Gulf, the Atlantic conjugate rifts) and the deep-ocean void. The cascade predicts the geography of oil.
