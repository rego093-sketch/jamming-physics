Module 52 — Tier-4 mainstream stress-test: every load-bearing claim against the strongest objection

1. The rules of this stress-test

Three commitments make this an honest adversarial test rather than a defence:

(a) Steelman, not strawman. Each mainstream objection is stated as its real positive case — the mechanism the mainstream actually offers, with its own evidence — not a weak caricature that is easy to dismiss. The constitution requires it: a favoured and a disfavoured interpretation of the same observation receive the same grade (GOVERNANCE, audit principle).

(b) The firewall cuts both ways. Where a mainstream objection rests on absolute chronology — "spreading took ~180 Myr", the radiometric calibration of magnetic stripes — that chronological form is [O] in both directions and is not counted against the cascade. Only the firewall-clean, present-tense content of each objection is adjudicated. Equally, the cascade may not promote occurrence; it stays [O].

(c) A refutation is a finding. The aim is not to make the cascade win. If a load-bearing step concedes, that is recorded as a result and localised, not explained away. The single most valuable output of this module is knowing exactly where the framework is most exposed.

4. What the tally shows

The deterministic counts: 7 DEGENERATE, 3 CONCEDE, 3 COHERENCE-ONLY, 1 OPEN, 1 OPEN-BY-DESIGN. Two facts matter most.

The central invariant — zero wins. Not one distinctive claim emerges with a discriminating present-tense advantage over the mainstream. Every axis the cascade might have won on (the petroleum-geography keystone M41, the salt-extension covariation M39, the superposition order M48, the source-rock facies M37, the forebulge geometry M35) is degenerate — the mainstream predicts the same present-tense result. This is the Module 20 structural-degeneracy finding, now confirmed against the mainstream's explicit best mechanisms rather than asserted. The script asserts this invariant (no DISCRIMINATING_WIN verdict exists) and fails if it is ever violated.

The concentrated point of failure — one root. All three CONCEDE verdicts (C01, C04, C11) trace to a single step: whether the S0 reservoir can actually be released (and trigger the near-critical crust). The mainstream holds this implausible on flood timescales; the cascade holds it [O]. The framework's entire empirical risk is concentrated here — exactly what the weak-link audit (M29) and the reservoir-budget bound (M50) already located, now confirmed under adversarial pressure. The script asserts that every conceded step belongs to this root.

5. The verdict on the 8:1 value claim (C14)

The capstone (M47) rests the framework's value on an 8:1 causal compression: one untuned cause closing nine phenomena where the mainstream uses eight independent mechanisms. The strongest mainstream rebuttal lands: those eight mechanisms are each independently evidenced (orbital forcing, plate tectonics, oceanic anoxic events, glacial-isostatic adjustment, the petroleum system each carry their own present-tense support), so they are not free parameters; and the cascade's single cause rests on one enormous open assumption — the S0 release. The honest restatement: the cascade trades eight independently-evidenced mechanisms for one mechanism plus one enormous [O] release, and that release does the work the eight do while lacking their evidence. The 8:1 is a real compression in mechanism-count and fitted-parameter-count (zero tuned vs many), and it is defensible as an [L] aesthetic preference — but it is not an epistemic advantage, and a rigorous mainstream reviewer is entitled to decline it. The v30 capstone tightening already states this (risk concentrated in SH-5); the stress-test confirms it.

6. What the stress-test does — and does not — establish

It does not refute the cascade. The framework never claimed occurrence; an honest tie on present-tense discriminators and a single [O] load-bearing step are exactly the standing it has always declared. Nor does it vindicate the mainstream's deep-time reading: that reading is also not forced by the firewall-clean present-tense data alone — the same degeneracy cuts both ways. What it establishes is a clean, externally-audited map of the terrain: the cascade is permitted and coherent, wins nothing present-tense, and is exposed at precisely one point. That is the most useful thing an adversarial test can produce, and it is recorded without flinching. Falsification = discovery.
