## 1. Forward predictions (falsifiable)

- **DP1 — two-kind material partition generalises [L].** Read further orthologous gene sets across the six taxa, or add taxa within each kind: the per-gene material should keep partitioning the two kinds, within-kind spread ≪ between-kind gap. *Fails if* a gene shows within-kind spread comparable to the between-kind gap on long (>1 kb) windows.
- **DP2 — decoupling holds out-of-sample [L].** On new gene-pairs (other clades, other loci), r(substitutions, |Δγ|) should stay ≈ 0 on length-matched windows. *Fails if* substitution count predicts |Δγ| (|r| ≳ 0.5) on a clean, length-controlled sample.
- **DP3 — adaptation is STATE-localised [L].** Other documented within-kind adaptations should read as a *small, localized* set of coding/state changes on a materially conserved locus. *Fails if* a documented within-kind adaptation requires a kind-scale γ shift.
- **DP4 — bistable states recur [L].** Switch-like phenotypes should read as two settings of one R19 bistable (small |Δγ|, localized coding changes), occasionally both present in one kind. *Fails if* such alternatives demand materially distinct loci.
- **DP5 — long-window convergence [F-leaning].** As read-window length grows, within-kind γ-range should fall toward the inherited < 0.0016 scale. *Fails if* long windows retain inflated within-kind range.

## 2. Honest open items (carried, not hidden)

- **Pooled non-bimodality (Module 02).** The two-kind partition is per-gene, not in the pooled γ. A cleaner cross-gene normalisation (e.g. per-gene-centred γ) might or might not yield a genuinely bimodal pooled distribution — *open*, and not currently claimed.
- **Within-kind window noise (Module 05).** Within-kind reads should use long windows; short genes are uninformative about within-kind material. A formal noise model for γ vs L would quantify the floor — *open, forward*.
- **Scope of taxa.** Two kinds, six genomes. The instrument is general; the *generalisation* of the two-kind result to other "past/present" pairs is DP1/DP3, not yet run.

## 3. The standing [O] — reserved, not pending

The histories — a flood between the two animals, or a deep-time gradual descent between them — are **[O] in both directions** and are **not** items this program will "complete." Under the firewall (GOVERNANCE Art. 2), that decision is reserved for **independent evidence** (the same posture the parent geodynamics volume takes toward the U–Pb break-up ages: conceded at full strength, chronology-agnostic). No future DNA read in this line can promote either history; it can only sharpen the present-tense material picture and the channel structure.
