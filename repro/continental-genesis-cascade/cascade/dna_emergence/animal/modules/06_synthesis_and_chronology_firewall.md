## 1. What the three reads say together

1. **Two kinds, in the material (H1, [V]).** Mammoth reads as the same material as living elephants; Neanderthal and Denisovan read as the same material as living humans — per gene, 13/13, median 17× separation. *In the material channel, "past animal" and "present animal" of a kind read as one kind.*
2. **Clock decoupled from material (H2, [V]).** The substitution count — the average theory's deep-time axis — does not predict the material the locus is made of (r = −0.08). The clock is a *different channel*, not a material/age read-out.
3. **Adaptive difference is STATE (H3, [V]).** On cold haemoglobin and the coat switch the material is conserved and the difference is a small, localized, *switchable* coding change — with one bistable caught in both states.

## 2. What this establishes — and what it cannot

**Establishes (present tense):** each pair is **one material kind** ([V]); the average theory's clock and the material are **different channels** ([V]); adaptation lives in **STATE** ([V]). These are measured, reproducible, and silent on history by construction.

**Cannot establish (the firewall, both directions):**

- The material reading is **present-tense**; it does not, and under the firewall cannot, decide *history*.
- H2 shows the **clock cannot carry a deep-time descent claim to the material** — but no result here shows a flood occurred. The decoupling **demotes the clock** as a history instrument; it does **not elevate** any alternative (GOVERNANCE Art. 2.2).
- Therefore **both** "a flood between them" **and** "a deep-time gradual descent between them" remain **[O]**. This is not hedging; it is forced by the data structure — the very decoupling that removes the clock's standing also denies this volume the right to assert the opposite.

## 3. The genuine contribution

The defensible win is **not** a chronology. It is:

1. a clean **two-kind material partition** (per gene, 13/13);
2. a measured **clock/material decoupling** (r ≈ 0, n = 78) — *the average theory's instrument and the material instrument are different channels*;
3. a literature-confirmed **STATE face** (the difference the environment sets lives in a switch, and one bistable is observed in both states).

That is what "doubt the average theory" yields when made to face NCBI data: **a measured decoupling, not a louder assertion.** It is the biological sibling of the Cascade's geophysical result — recent-sequence biology reads as one material kind per pair with the clock decoupled from the material, and likewise leaves occurrence open.
