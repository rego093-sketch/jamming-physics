# GOVERNANCE — the VP Plant Environment DNA-Emergence constitution

This document fixes the rules this volume is built under. It is the plant-face statement of the VP-SPEC discipline, inherited from the foundation, the inherited **DNA interpretation method**, and the sibling DNA-Emergence (animals) and Cascade (geophysics) volumes.

---

## Article 1 — The question, and the occurrence cap

**1.1** This volume asks what the **inherited material instrument** measures when applied to real plant genomes: whether the readable material (γ) is set by the **environment** (cold vs UV) or by the **lineage/kind**, and whether the molecular clock is the same channel as the material.

**1.2 — Occurrence cap.** Any statement that a particular **past environment or history occurred** — a "past cold greenhouse," a flood, a deep-time descent — is capped at **[O] (open)**. Only *present-tense measurement* and *mechanism* may be graded higher.

**1.3** Which history occurred is reserved for independent evidence, not decided by the material reading.

---

## Article 2 — The bidirectional chronology firewall

**2.1** Absolute chronology and any descent-history premise are **forbidden as load-bearing in both directions.** The volume neither asserts "recent / a past greenhouse" nor "deep time." Both stay **[O]**.

**2.2** The central result — that the environment does **not** write the readable material (γ is a lineage property) — is a *present-tense* statement about where the environment acts. It does **not** license any history claim in either direction.

**2.3** The inherited DNA method is **evolution-/phylogeny-silent by construction**; it reads present-tense material only. This volume adopts that silence and never converts a material reading into a descent, age, or past-environment statement.

---

## Article 3 — Data grading (what may be load-bearing)

| Tier | Meaning | Load-bearing? |
|---|---|---|
| **G0** | a real, fetchable NCBI accession (a deposited sequence) | **yes (ACTIVE)** |
| **G1** | a deterministic, no-tuning read of G0 (γ from the LOCK table; a CDS extraction; an alignment substitution count; GC fraction) | **yes (ACTIVE)** |
| **G2** | a model-light contrast of G1 quantities (between-kind gap/spread; cross-channel correlation; γ↔GC correlation) | yes (ACTIVE), flagged |
| **G3** | a literature/annotation cross-reference used only to *label* a locus (which gene is the cold switch) | corroboration only |
| **G4** | a model-dependent reconstruction (a tree, a calibrated clock, an ancestral state, a divergence date) | **record only** |
| **G5** | an absolute date / molecular-clock age | **record only** |

A claim may be promoted to [F]/[V] only on G0–G2. The molecular-clock *count* in §3 is a G1 read (a raw substitution tally); its *interpretation as an age* is G5 and never load-bearing.

---

## Article 4 — No-tuning, single substrate, inherited-instrument integrity

**4.1 — No-tuning.** The material engine's only constants are the inherited SantaLucia (1998) LOCK table; γ and the R19 thresholds derive from it with no free parameter. SEED = 19; every computation is deterministic and scripted.

**4.2 — Single substrate.** One engine — the **R19 jammed ⇄ unjammed bistable switch** — underlies every read: γ sets the switch threshold; the cold switch (CBF) and UV switch (CHS/UVR8) are the inventory the environment deploys; γ-conservation is the substrate read across taxa.

**4.3 — Inherited-instrument integrity.** The DNA reading is **not re-tuned here**; it is vendored faithfully from the inherited method (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20471407). Modifying it would define a new method, not tune this one.

**4.4 — Build-then-grade, anti-contamination.** Each emergence is built first, then graded; honest qualifications are recorded at the end of each step. Where the data refines the naive hypothesis (γ is a lineage property, not "any cold gene = any UV gene"), the refinement is **stated in the open** and the over-claim is explicitly refused.

---

## Article 5 — Verdict scheme

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[F]** | forced / derived — follows from the LOCK table + the engine |
| **[V]** | verified — measured directly on a real accession, or reproduced by the SEED-fixed engine |
| **[L]** | leaning — a well-anchored inference / forward prediction |
| **[O]** | open — undecided; includes **all past-environment / history claims in both directions** |

Precedence **[F] > [V] > [L] > [O]**. Nothing launders an [O] history claim upward.

---

## Article 6 — Reproducibility obligation

**6.1** Every load-bearing read is a runnable, SEED-fixed, no-tuning script over a **frozen real NCBI record**; every load-bearing observation is a fetchable accession. The mapping is in `REPRODUCIBILITY_MAP.md`; accessions in `repro/data/accessions.json`; live re-fetch in `repro/fetch_ncbi.py`.

**6.2** The whole spine reproduces from `repro/run_all.py` with **no network** and **no fitted parameter**.

**6.3 — 반증 = 발견.** Three honest findings are recorded openly, not hidden: (a) the plant plastid material does **not** strongly partition the two kinds the way animal mtDNA did (it is conserved across kinds); (b) cross-lineage CHS γ varies substantially, so "any cold gene = any UV gene" is **refused**; (c) γ is a composition (GC) measure, which is why it is a lineage property and bounds how differences may be read. These sharpen the result and are part of the contribution.

---

*These rules are fixed for this volume. The material reading speaks in the present tense and stays there; the environment is shown to act on STATE/inventory/dwell, not on the material; and any past environment or history is held open by construction, in both directions.*
