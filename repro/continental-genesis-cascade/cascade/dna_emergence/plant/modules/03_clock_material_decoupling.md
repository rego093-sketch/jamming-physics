# Module 03 — The clock and the material are decoupled in plants too (H2)

**Author:** Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
**Tier:** emergence · **Inherits:** Module 02 · **Status:** measured on 372 within-kind gene-pairs · **Grade:** [V]

**One line.** The molecular clock and the material are different physical channels — the same result as in animals, now in an independent kingdom.

---

On the same plastid genes, within each kind, across **all 372 within-kind gene-pairs**, we read both channels (`repro/clock_decoupling.py`): |Δγ| (material) and substitution count (the molecular clock — the average theory's deep-time axis).

**Result.** **Pearson r(substitutions, |Δγ|) = +0.089 ≈ 0.** The clock counts **9245 substitutions** in total; γ moves a mean of only **0.0058** on the ~1.28 scale, and not in proportion. The clock and the material **do not co-vary** — they are different channels. A clock distance therefore cannot be promoted to a material or age statement here, exactly as the animal volume found.

This is an **independent-kingdom confirmation** of the decoupling: same engine, different organisms, same answer.

**Grade [V]:** clock/material decoupling reproduced in plants (r = +0.09, n = 372). The clock-as-age reading remains G5 / [O] by the firewall.
