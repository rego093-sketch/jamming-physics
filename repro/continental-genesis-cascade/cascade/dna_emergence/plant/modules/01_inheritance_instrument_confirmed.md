# Module 01 — The inherited material instrument (fixed, not tuned)

**Author:** Young Jae Lee · ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245 · CC BY 4.0 · https://jamming-physics.org/
**Tier:** instrument · **Inherits:** VP DNA interpretation method (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20471407), VP physics (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17932566)
**Status:** reproduced in-environment · **Grade:** [V]

**One line.** The same evolution-silent material engine used for animals is vendored here unchanged and fed real plants.

---

The material engine reads any locus as **γ = −mean(NN stacking ΔG)** over the SantaLucia (1998) nearest-neighbour LOCK table, strand-symmetric, with the locus's **R19 bistable switch** scale derived from γ alone. There is **no fitted parameter** (SEED = 19); `repro/vp_gamma_engine.py` self-tests to a deterministic γ.

Two properties matter for this volume. First, the method is **evolution-/phylogeny-silent by construction** — it reads *present-tense material*, never a descent or age. That is exactly what lets it test *where the environment writes* without smuggling in a history model. Second, it is **inherited, not re-tuned**: modifying it would define a new method, not tune this one. We apply it to real plant genomes and report what it measures.

**Grade [V]:** instrument inherited and reproduced; no tuning; evolution-silent posture preserved.
