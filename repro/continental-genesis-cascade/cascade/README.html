What this is (and is not)

Single source of truth & easy door (v30): the canonical narrative is the whitepaper; the no-jargon on-ramp is Start here; every term is in the glossary; the petroleum strand’s single source of truth is the Glaciation-Driven Petroleum overview. This package is v30 (adds the glaciation-driven petroleum strand M33–M44, the Continental-Genesis import M45, the two reframes, a plain-language layer, and one-command reproducibility).

This package presents one question, built out in full and graded honestly:

Is a single coupled relaxation — a flood, a glaciation, and an ocean-opening, all driven by one release of a jammed deep-water reservoir — physically permitted by measured constants and the VP jamming engine?

It is: a unified construction on a single substrate (jammed State-4 water), driven by a single engine (void-suction + unjamming) that is inherited from a separate, reproducible geodynamics volume and re-verified here, presenting as a coupled three-face relaxation with a closing energy ledger and five falsifiable predictions.

It is not: a claim that the sequence occurred; a claim about any absolute timescale; or a comparison against alternative geological frameworks. The last — the "stress-test" — is deliberately deferred to a later phase. This package builds the cascade on its own terms and is explicit about what it does and does not establish.

Governance (why the grades can be trusted)

The cascade is built under the VP-SPEC constitution, the same discipline as the inherited volumes:

  • No-tuning. No parameter is fitted to make a result come out; every number is a measured input or a derived consequence (SEED = 19 convention).
  • Graded verdicts. [F] forced/derived · [V] verified (measured / engine-reproduced) · [L] leaning (well-anchored inference) · [O] open. Precedence forced > verified > leaning > open; nothing downstream may launder an [O] into a higher grade.
  • Bidirectional chronology firewall. Statements about a past occurrence are capped at [O] in both directions — neither "deep time" nor "recent" is load-bearing. Absolute dates are held as record only. The inherited geodynamics volume concedes the U–Pb break-up ages at full strength and stays chronology-agnostic; the cascade adopts that posture exactly.
  • Single substrate. One engine (the R19 jammed ⇄ unjammed bistable switch) underlies every step; the cascade does not introduce a new mechanism per phenomenon.
  • Falsification = discovery. An honest negative is a finding, not a failure; corrections are recorded in the open (see the module ledger).

What the integrated package now carries (beyond the cascade)

Per author directive, this package now contains, runnable and graded:

  • The DNA Emergence, inherited whole (dna_emergence/). Opening this whitepaper, a reader can actually reproduce the DNA emergence — animal and plant — from frozen NCBI records, no network, no tuning. It establishes three present-tense facts (material is a lineage property; the molecular clock is decoupled from the material; the environment writes to STATE not material) and, by those same facts, shows DNA is neutral on the occurrence: it neither proves deep time nor supports a flood — it exits the elapsed-time question in both directions (Module 22).
  • The fossil-count audit (fossil_audit/), an open strand. Raw counts recomputed from 179,689 real radiocarbon dates: ancient remains are genuinely sparse ([V]), but the counts cannot, alone, attribute that sparseness to any cause ([O]). No external model is imported (Module 23).
  • Article 7 — audit of interpretation and data (GOVERNANCE.md; Module 24). The standing rule, with its live recorded case: hold every interpretation (including the mainstream one) to its grade symmetrically, and recompute every datum from source rather than recalling it. The willingness to refuse a borrowed explanation and to recompute a number — even against one's own prior output — is itself part of the constitution.

This package establishes a physically-permitted, occurrence-open cascade with a reproducible spine and an honestly-graded ledger — the object its constitution prescribes. It does not claim the sequence happened, and it does not (here) claim superiority over any alternative. Those are separate, pre-registered questions.
