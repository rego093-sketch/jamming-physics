# CONSTITUTION — cardioresp_vp_site

The governing articles of this package. On any conflict, **C0 (single deliverable + English body)**
and the **firewall (A1)** override every other clause. This document is inherited in spirit from the
`analgesic_threshold_logic` constitution and is binding on every future session that edits the package.

---

## A1 — The firewall (mechanism ≠ clinic)

This package models cardiorespiratory **mechanism dynamics** — relaxation oscillators, delayed control
loops, and R19 bistable switches. It is **not** a clinical, pharmacological, diagnostic, or treatment
artifact, and it asserts **no** therapy, dose, drug, efficacy, or safety claim anywhere.

- **γ is a promoter stacking-stability read, never a clinical magnitude.** The master-gene γ values
  (NKX2-5, NKX2-1) fix organ *identity* and developmental *order* (cited from DNA, grade [V]); they are
  never equated with a heart rate, a channel voltage, a drug potency, a dose, or an incidence rate.
- Every **absolute** physiological/epidemiological magnitude (organ size, absolute relative risk, PC20,
  Pcrit, capsaicin C5, absolute febrile rate) is graded **[O]** and listed in
  `IRREPRODUCIBILITY_LEDGER.md` with its obstacle. The **shapes and directions** are the forced content.
- The firewall is enforced mechanically by the **fail-closed forbidden-claim scanner**
  (`repro/_verify/claim_scanner.py`, gate R7): any drug-dosing, prescriptive, treatment-of-disease, or
  efficacy/safety language on the canonical site fails the build.

## A2 — Honest grading

Every displayed quantity carries a grade: **[F]** forced by the R19 substrate · **[V]** reproduced and
numerically checked · **[L]** anchored to a cited literature value · **[O]** open (needs external
calibration). An **[O] without a stated obstacle is a gate FAIL** (gate R9 cross-checks every site [O]
against the ledger). Only these four grades may appear on the canonical site (gate R8). Self-deprecating
language is not used; the grading vocabulary is the honesty mechanism, not a tone.

## A3 — No tuning

No constant is ever chosen to hit a target. Every number is a measured input (cited) or a derived value
(forced by the substrate). Discriminants pass under wide sweeps with **no per-target tuning**, or they do
not pass.

## A4 — Research first, writing second

The canonical site (`docs/`) is produced only after the research is signed off. `tools/build_docs.py`
**refuses** while `gates.writing_locked()` is True. Unlock requires all three: the research gate green,
`reports/research_complete.json` written, and `PHASE == writing`. Nothing with an *anticipated* grade
(see `FUTURE_WORK.md`) enters `docs/`.

## A5 — Determinism (C1)

Every number on the site is regenerated by a deterministic engine: BLAS pinned single-thread, fixed
seed, round-before-hash, sorted JSON keys. Two engine runs yield an identical sha256 (gate R1); the full
report blob is byte-identical across recomputation (**drift 0**, gate R2). The build is **wall-clock
free** (`RELEASE_DATE` is pinned), so regenerating the site reproduces it byte-for-byte. The whole
package is pinned by a SHA256 manifest (`manifest/SHA256SUMS.txt`, gate R6) and reproduces with the
network disabled (gate R11).

## A6 — Reproducibility technology (inherited from analgesic_threshold_logic v2.0)

The reproducibility apparatus is a first-class deliverable, not an afterthought. It comprises: a
one-command harness (`repro/run_all.py`) that reports `OVERALL: PASS (N/N), drift 0`; a file-level
SHA256 manifest with freeze/verify; the fail-closed claim scanner; the grade-legality, ledger, and DOI
integrity gates; per-gate JSON snapshots under `reports/`; and a deposit-ready whitepaper
(`zenodo/`). This technology is to be **inherited by every sibling package** (see `FUTURE_WORK.md` §8).
