# IRREPRODUCIBILITY LEDGER

Every `[O]` (open) quantity is listed here with its specific obstacle (VP-SPEC C3). An `[O]` without a
stated obstacle is a gate FAIL. Each item is cross-checked against the body text that carries it.

| item | grade | obstacle (why not reproducible in-package) | location |
|---|---|---|---|
| absolute organ size / mass | [O] | DWELL ∝ γ^1.5 fixes RELATIVE size and developmental order [F]/[V]; the absolute magnitude needs an external calibration not contained in the substrate geometry | engine (organ emergence); §1, §9 |
| absolute cancer relative-risk magnitude | [O] | the dose–response SHAPE (RR(0)=1, no threshold, convex) is forced/verified [F]/[V], but the absolute RR at a given exposure depends on the switch noise temperature (`scale`) and the molecular dose→bias conversion (`kappa`), neither of which is fixed by substrate geometry; both require an external population-hazard calibration — exactly as absolute organ size is open while size order is forced | repro/_oncology; §8, §9 |
| absolute cardiorespiratory disease-spectrum magnitudes | [O] | across the shared-substrate spectrum the mechanism reuse is verified [V] (Mayer-wave resonance, airway-tone bistability, the obstructive-apnea loop-gain endotype, the periodic-breathing Hopf spectrum, fever HR–RR co-scaling, all-or-none cough), but every absolute magnitude (provocation level, collapse pressure, periodic-breathing thresholds, co-scaling slope, excitation threshold) depends on the same switch noise scale and stimulus→bias conversion that are not fixed by substrate geometry and need an external calibration — the same obstacle as absolute organ size and absolute cancer relative-risk | repro/_disease; §11 |

Notes
- These three `[O]` rows are the package's only open items. In every case the forced/verified counterpart
  (size ORDER; dose–response SHAPE and the no-threshold endpoint; the reused dynamical mechanism) stands
  independently of the open absolute scale.
- All other displayed quantities are graded [F] (forced by the R19 substrate), [V] (reproduced and
  checked numerically), or [L] (anchored to a cited literature value), and carry no obstacle.
