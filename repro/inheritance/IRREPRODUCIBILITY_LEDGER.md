# IRREPRODUCIBILITY LEDGER — VP Inheritance Kit

Per VP-SPEC C3, every **[O]** item names its obstacle. These are direction-only by construction: the R19
substrate fixes *which* switch, the *sign* of the drive, and the *ordering/decay* of effects — it does
**not** fix any absolute magnitude. Promoting an [O] item requires new *measured* dynamics, never a louder
claim. Every clinical application is firewalled to clinicians and regulators.

## Honest-negative / null policy
Pre-registered tests are reported **as they fall**. A battery that only passes for a hand-chosen γ is a
failed battery, not a tuned success — the γ is never moved to rescue a result. Negative and null outcomes
are recorded, not hidden. No result is graded [V] on the strength of an unmeasured magnitude.

## (B) validation — held-out score + identifiability stress test + decomposition + sign-law `−` and `+` arms (battery FV1–FV8)
The **(B)** crossing named throughout O-19/O-20/O-22 (and in FM4/DM4) has now been **run once**, on the
RNA/disease ordering axis, and is recorded here under the honest-negative policy. It is the first time the
kit scores its no-tuning **(A)-map γ-ordering** against a **measured, genuinely held-out** target.

- **Target (held out):** Replogle et al. 2022 *Cell* genome-scale Perturb-seq (CRISPRi), DOI
  `10.1016/j.cell.2022.05.013`, figshare article `20029387`. Readout = `obs/fold_expr`, the **on-target
  fraction of transcript remaining** after knockdown. The kit's γ is measured **purely from promoter DNA**
  (NN-stacking ΔG37, GRCh38 TSS−2000..+500) and was **frozen before any expression data was seen**, so the
  CRISPRi readout carries none of the information that produced γ — a legitimate held-out target.
- **Panel (fixed rule, declared before scoring):** union of all seven frozen γ atlases (neuro, onco,
  neurodegen, rna-carrier, imprint, germline, immune), deduplicated by symbol, matched to perturbations by
  exact HGNC symbol with the single documented alias GBA1→GBA. Primary set = **K562 genome-wide** (n=43
  matched); robustness = RPE1 (n=11) and K562-essential (n=7).
- **Prediction (sign-locked before scoring):** a deeper-barrier (higher-γ) promoter resists the knockdown
  drive ⇒ higher γ ⇒ more transcript remaining ⇒ **Spearman ρ(γ, fold_expr) > 0**.
- **Result (reported as it falls — NULL):** ρ = **−0.0760**, p = **0.6283**, n = **43** (primary). The point
  estimate is *slightly opposite* to the predicted positive sign and far from significance; it stays
  non-significant and slightly negative across **every** control-expression cutoff (−0.08 → −0.33, all
  p ≥ 0.29); the 2nd cell line's sign **disagrees** (RPE1 ρ = +0.21, p = 0.53); the threshold-free SET-proxy
  AUC = **0.389** (near chance, slightly anti-predictive). γ was **re-read frozen** and hash-checked against
  the atlases; **no parameter or γ was tuned** to the target.
- **What it changes:** **nothing is promoted.** Because the held-out score is not significant in the
  predicted direction, the promotion rule (p<0.05 **and** ρ>0) keeps **O-19, O-20, O-22 at `[O]`**. The
  **(A)** map (FM1/DM reachability=spinodal, ordering by frozen γ) is untouched and stays `[V]`. The null is
  **low-power** (n=43/11): it does **not refute** the map, it finds **no support** for promoting the per-cell
  / per-patient yes/no. Notably it is **consistent with FM4**: the part the self-contained sim can compute
  (the γ-ordering) does not, by itself, predict the firewalled per-cell effect.
- **Confound, now STRESS-TESTED → the null is NON-IDENTIFIED (battery FV5):** FV4 *recorded* that γ is
  entangled with promoter GC; **FV5 stress-tests it instead of noting it.** Because γ = −mean(NN-stacking
  ΔG37) and stacking free energy is dominated by G/C content, γ and promoter GC are near-collinear **by
  construction** — measured here at Spearman ρ(γ,GC) = **0.9909 / 0.9818 / 0.9643** on the three panels. The
  held-out readout (on-target CRISPRi `fold_expr`) is in turn driven by sgRNA accessibility, a known function
  of TSS chromatin/GC. So the **predictor (γ) and the readout's nuisance driver (GC) are the same ordering.**
  Partialling GC out, the surviving γ effect is **non-significant and sign-unstable** across panels
  (ρ(γ,fold_expr|GC) = **+0.126 / −0.223 / −0.428**, all p ≥ 0.40; no panel shows an identified positive). So
  the recorded null is the **stronger, measured statement** "the knockdown-depth ordering score **cannot
  separate a barrier effect from a GC/accessibility effect**" — *non-identified*, not merely *signal-absent*.
  FV5 promotes nothing; O-19/O-20/O-22 stay `[O]` with this sharpened obstacle.
- **Consequence for the open neuronal (B) — settled without new data:** the γ↔GC collinearity is a
  **promoter-DNA property, identical in any cell type** (K562, RPE1, or any neuron). Therefore scoring the
  **same observable** (on-target knockdown depth vs γ) on a neuronal CRISPRi/Perturb-seq panel — e.g. the
  publicly available i³Neuron CRISPRi CROP-seq data (Tian et al. 2019, GEO `GSE124703`; CRISPRbrain) — would
  **inherit the identical non-identification**. The neuronal (B) for **O-20** is thus **not advanced** by
  repeating the knockdown-depth ordering in neurons (the data exist; the *test* is what is degenerate). An
  **identified** (B) needs a readout **not collinear with promoter GC** — a downstream **response magnitude**
  (how far the cell's state moved), which requires the **firewalled drive size Δh**. So the
  self-contained-decidable (B) axis is non-identified, and the identifiable (B) axis is firewalled.
- **Still genuinely open (unchanged):** the **SET-membership precision/recall** (needs an operating Δh) and
  the **corrective sign-law DM2** (needs disease-vs-normal corrective pre/post data with a restore arm — a
  *knockdown screen in a leukemia line cannot test LOF→`+`restore / GOF→`−`silence*). Reproduced offline +
  deterministically by battery **FV** (engine/`feasibility_validation.py`) from the vendored slice
  `bvalidation/replogle2022_heldout.cache.json`; rebuildable from source via `data/fetch_bvalidation.py
  --fetch`. **No γ or cached datum was tuned — FV5 adds only analysis (the cache is byte-identical).**
