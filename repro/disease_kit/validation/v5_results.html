What is orthogonal, stated honestly. MED-RT (NLM/VA, formerly NDF-RT) curates Mechanism-of-Action from FDA Structured Product Labels — it is not ChEMBL and not a DGIdb source, so the DIRECTION annotation has no ChEMBL/DGIdb lineage. V5 orthogonalises the direction annotation, not the (drug↔gene) membership: the approved drugs per gene are inherited from V4 (ChEMBL-derived); V5 only swaps the source that labels each drug an inhibitor vs an activator. So V5 rebuts “the agreement is an artifact of ChEMBL’s actionType vocabulary” — the one bound V4 left open — no more.
