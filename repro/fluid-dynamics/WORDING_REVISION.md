# Wording revision — confidence without overclaim (plan items 1–5)

Goal: a paradigm-declaring whitepaper should state its genuine achievements at
full strength and not bury them under apologetic hedging — while keeping the
*accurate* scope boundaries, because erasing true limits is overclaiming and
backfires in review.

Distinction applied throughout: **strengthen genuine under-selling; keep the two
real open gates, but state them as precisely-delimited frontiers rather than as
deficits.**

## Changes
1. **§14 reframed.** The two "non-claim" items were rewritten:
   - "We do not overturn the Navier–Stokes equations… we derive them" →
     **"The Navier–Stokes equations are derived, not overturned."** (Pillar I,
     stated as the achievement it is, foundation *beneath* fluid dynamics).
   - "We do not claim a proof of global regularity… *reframing is not proving*"
     → **"Global regularity is compressed to one gate, not claimed."** The
     configurational contribution (smoothing at finite kernel width; events as a
     physical regularization valve) is stated confidently; the open part is named
     as one sharply-stated zero-width-limit gate, not apologized for. The
     apologetic clause "reframing is not proving" was removed.
   - Abstract: "We claim…" → **"We establish… the entire residual is compressed
     to two named gates."**
2. **§14 / §01 / hub descriptions and abstracts** restated in declarative,
   paradigm-forward language ("forced four ways from a single measured
   structure", "derived, not assumed").
3. **§01 opening** sharpened ("makes one move and follows it to the end";
   "are all forced by that structure"). Tail non-claim → "the two open frontiers
   are named with equal precision" (keeps NS-derived, regularity-open,
   substrate-not-unique — all true — but confidently framed).
4. **Hub** intro: "reframes those facts" → **"shows those facts to be forced —
   derived as necessary consequences of arrangement."**
5. **New "Scope of coverage" paragraph (§01).** States the *breadth* explicitly
   (one premise → balance laws, fixed length, selected length, dissipation law,
   transition class) and compresses the unresolved part to **exactly two gates**
   (global regularity; infinite-Re flux-localization). Breadth and frontier are
   stated together, by design.

## What was deliberately NOT changed (integrity line)
The two genuine open gates remain open: (a) global regularity in the zero-width
limit, and (b) the infinite-Reynolds flux-localization of the dissipation
mechanism. "Deriving the equations" and "proving regularity of their solutions"
are different theorems; the document continues to distinguish them. The A0
non-uniqueness, the no-numeric-adjacency-identity guard, and the non-import of
the companion's speed-of-light reading are kept. These guards protect
credibility and are themselves a strength.
