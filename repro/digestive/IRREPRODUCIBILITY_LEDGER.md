### Falsifiable predictions (NOT open items — stated for the record)
- **Gastric Hp x diet sub-multiplicativity.** The exact-barrier kernel reproduces the cited *super-additive* H. pylori x dietary-nitroso synergy [V] and, on the same kernel, predicts the joint RR is *sub-multiplicative* near the spinodal (diminishing returns at extreme dual exposure). This is a concrete, falsifiable departure from a multiplicative-null interaction model.
- **Diabetes: capacity loss is catastrophic, gain loss is compensated (§12).** On one homeostat, β-cell capacity depletion and insulin-sensitivity loss are *not* interchangeable: at matched perturbation depth the capacity deficit always yields the higher fasting glucose and the gap *widens* with depth — capacity loss runs away past the diabetes threshold (setpoint lost), gain loss saturates at a stable elevated setpoint (return preserved). The widening matched-depth gap is the falsifiable signature.
- **Peptic-ulcer site is a barrier-route split (§13).** Gastric and duodenal ulcers reach the same ulcer basin by *distinct* routes on one barrier — gastric by reduced defense scale g (at moderate acid), duodenal by raised acid bias h (at intact defense). The prediction: defense-directed vs acid-directed interventions should differentially shift the two sites.
- **Colonic inertia is the sub-threshold collapse of slow-transit (§14).** Slow-transit constipation and colonic inertia are one propulsive-drive axis: above a threshold transit is graded and prokinetic-responsive, below it transport collapses and a modest drive boost stays sub-threshold (refractory). The prediction: there is a drive threshold separating prokinetic-responsive from prokinetic-refractory disease (the latter the surgical-escalation regime), and ileus (a *transient* drive loss) is reversible whereas inertia (a *fixed* sub-threshold state) is not.
- **One ICC-depletion lesion across organs (§11+§14, unifying hypothesis #1).** A single pacemaker-density parameter collapses gastric emptying (§11) and intestinal transit (§14) along nearly the same curve. The prediction: gastroparesis, slow-transit constipation, and chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction share one cause (ICC depletion) expressed at different sites — pan-gut ICC loss should co-segregate these phenotypes.
- **Dumping is a delivery-speed phenomenon on one glucose loop (§15).** The same carbohydrate load delivered faster produces a higher early glucose peak and, with an exaggerated incretin insulin response, a biphasic late reactive-hypoglycaemia crossing 3.9 mM — both monotone in delivery speed and robust across loads. The prediction: slowing carbohydrate delivery (smaller, lower-glycaemic meals; reservoir / pyloric-brake restoration) flattens the peak and abolishes the late nadir, and early and late dumping are two readings of one loop rather than separate diseases.
- **Insulinoma is the exact mirror of type-1 diabetes on the homeostat (§15+§12).** An autonomous insulin *source* drives fasting glucose below threshold exactly as a missing insulin *arm* drives it above — one loop, opposite sign — and removing the source returns the loop to setpoint. The prediction: the disease is source-driven (resection-curable) rather than a setpoint defect, and reactive hypoglycaemia shares its undershoot mechanism with late dumping.
- **Functional dyspepsia (motility) and gastroparesis are one ICC axis at different depths (§15+§11).** A mild amplitude reduction gives a mild emptying-rate delay distinct from the severe gastroparesis collapse on the *same* parameter.
