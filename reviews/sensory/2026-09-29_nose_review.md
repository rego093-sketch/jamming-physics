# Review — nose (Olfactory Emergence), 2026-09-29

Scope: light review. `docs/nose/` (hub + §0–§5), `repro/nose/`, `docs/nose/_decl.json`. No page or code was edited.

## Summary
- The code runs, is deterministic, and prints exactly the numbers on the pages. The volume gate passes (HTML↔code drift 0) once `docs/` is found. There is no wave module, and there are no clinical magnitudes anywhere.
- Three headline numbers come from choices in the scripts, not from the physics: the thermometer's "4 of 128" (it is 8 with a continuous sweep), the "≈229×" steepness (it depends on the step width chosen), and the "byte-identical" CNGB1 match (the same promoter measured twice by the same code). Several [V] grades are true by construction and are not falsification tests.
- §3 says that developmental order is "forced by measured γ" [F]. That breaks the corpus rule that order comes from DNA-atlas cascade depth, not γ. §3's "one receptor → one glomerulus bijection" does not match known anatomy.
