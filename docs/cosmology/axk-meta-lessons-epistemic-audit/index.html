What happened (epistemic audit)

Of the thirteen problems, one closed analytically (#3, the geometric 2π); several advanced from “mechanism only” to a reproduced number (#5, #6, #9, #10); two advanced from “qualitative” or “not derived” to a forced structure (#11 acceleration feasibility, #12 the locking dichotomy); one was reframed from a flat conflict to an open dynamical item (#2); one remains an explicit open input (#4, absolute G); and one was returned as an honest negative (#13, solar activity). No problem was closed by adjusting a definition, a value, or a gate—the audit trail is the set of scripts named in Appendix (versionhistory), each deterministic and tied to its page's slug.

Meta-lessons for the next reviewer

  • L1. Three honest outcomes are distinct. Mechanism-shown (a process is demonstrated), value-computed (a number is reproduced from physical parameters), and honest-negative (the framework cannot do it) are different grades and are labelled separately throughout. Conflating them is the most common way a framework over-claims.
  • L2. A steady cause cannot clock a periodic effect. The decisive content of #13: the solar cycle is an oscillation, an oscillation needs an intrinsic frequency, and a steady inflow has no imaginary eigenvalue to supply one. Match the time-structure of cause and effect before proposing a mechanism.
  • L3. Forced constants are predictions; fitted constants are accommodations. The headline a₀=cH₀/2π is a prediction because the 2π is geometric; the scorecard's grade-beside-value design (Appendix (scorecard)) exists so this distinction cannot hide.
  • L4. Data-limited residuals are not mechanism failures. #6 (full χ²), #7/#8 (joint fit), #9 (η–δ degeneracy), and #10 (absolute value) are bounded by missing data, not by the framework, and are marked as such rather than presented as successes or failures.
  • L5. Out-of-scope is not unsolved. Cosmic origins (nucleosynthesis, the acoustic peaks, structure-formation history) are excluded by design; stating that plainly is more honest than manufacturing a closure the present-facts standard cannot support.
  • L6. Reproduce from the slug. Every quantitative claim ties to a named script under repro/cosmology/{slug}/ with the same slug as its page; the provenance ledger (Appendix (provenance)) marks each number geometry / simulation / measurement. A reviewer should start from the scorecard (Appendix (scorecard)) and follow the grade to its script.
