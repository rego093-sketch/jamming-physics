The honest negative

The one empirical attempt at an access marker — a perturbational-complexity read combining high-frequency (γ) and low-frequency activity to index consciousness — did not robustly reproduce. It is reported as a negative, not buried: the paper has no validated marker of consciousness, and says so.

Open register

  • the access question: what makes some processing reportable rather than silent — unresolved.
  • the hard problem: why any of the function is accompanied by experience — unresolved; the model specifies function only (§7, §8).
  • the felt quale: the subjective character of a thought or feeling — open.
  • the absolutes across the chain: selection time, eddy size, items per θ frame, learning rate, dopamine concentration.
  • the causal status of binding-by-synchrony itself: whether γ-coherence is causal to binding or a correlate is debated in neuroscience and is not settled here.

Retired register (irreversible)

The following stay retired, under any name. The electromagnetic “vortex field” as a quantum carrier of binding — the coherence-length argument that rejected it (short by ~10⁹×) is the quantum decoherence length, and validly refutes quantum theories of mind. Scope correction: that argument does not close the classical field, which §4 emerges to be coherent across the brain; so the classical-field-as-medium question was wrongly placed here and is reopened as open, to be decided by field strength (§2, §4) — what stays retired is the quantum carrier, not the classical-field question. The axon as an optical fibre / TIR waveguide, and the EEG as an electromagnetic carrier’s beat — both physically false (see the neuro paper, §9). Energy equated with information; a phase memory written in DNA. The functional parallel-eddy reading of this paper is a structure on the ionic substrate (§2).

What would move each claim

Honestly stated, the upgrades: a claim moves from model to direction when a wired mechanism’s direction is matched in real data (several already are, inherited from neuro); from direction to causal when a manipulation changes the outcome as predicted — for the stream, a phase-targeted intervention that changes selection or binding in a behaviour-labelled, artefact-controlled task. For consciousness specifically, an upgrade needs a robust, reproducible access marker plus report-labelled data — which does not yet exist. Until then, this remains a frontier paper.
