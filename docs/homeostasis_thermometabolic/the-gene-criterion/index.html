The gene criterion: a present, drivable effector-command pair

The gene-level criterion is NOT a γ value. Across the panel the endotherms all carry a drivable thermogenic effector-command pair -- a furnace (UCP1) plus a sympathetic command (ADRB3) -- while in both ectotherms queried the ADRB3 command does not resolve to a genomic ortholog. Presence of the drivable pair, observed not assumed, is the criterion.

A measured cross-species panel (UCP1 furnace, ADRB3 command, PDK4 fuel-switch; human, mouse, rat, pig, ground squirrel, zebrafish, frog) shows the command gene present across endotherms and absent in the ectotherms queried. The deeper criterion is the DYNAMICS -- a defended attractor -- because where the furnace is silenced (pig pseudogene) the endotherm still defends by other routes.

The criterion, stated

Endotherms have the drivable {UCP1 furnace + ADRB3 command} pair: True. The ectotherms queried lack the ADRB3 command (no ortholog resolves): True. The criterion is a PRESENT, drivable effector-command pair -- a structural fact about the genome, read by presence, not a number on the γ scale.

Why the deeper criterion is the dynamics

The pig is an endotherm whose UCP1 is a pseudogene, yet it still defends its setpoint by other routes. So the single furnace gene is neither necessary nor a γ threshold; the robust criterion is the DEFENDED ATTRACTOR itself, which the R19 loop captures directly. Two explicit NULLs make this firewall concrete (next chapter and the bear-vs-human chapter).
