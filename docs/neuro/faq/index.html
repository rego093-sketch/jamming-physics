Frequently asked questions

What is the one substrate the whole chain stands on?

The R19 bistable switch (ds/dt = g s − s³ + h), the jamming-lattice primitive that also writes DNA genes. A neuron is that switch plus a slow FitzHugh–Nagumo recovery — a low-frequency relaxation oscillator. Every cell in this volume runs the same switch; only the inherited measured γ changes.

What is the strongest result?

Working-memory capacity equals the θ/γ ratio, about 7±2, because each gamma cycle nested in one theta cycle holds one item. It is the one link with causal validation: manipulating theta with tACS changes the realised capacity in the predicted direction (§4).

Which pain conditions does this volume own?

Acquired, dynamics-key conditions read on the nociceptor firing threshold — neuropathic pain, sensitisation, inflammatory/nociplastic pain (§22). Monogenic pain channelopathies (inherited erythromelalgia, PEPD, CIP/CIPA) are gene-key entities owned by the rare-disease volume (disease_wp) and only cross-referenced (§23).

γ reads promoter switch-threshold STRUCTURE only — never a channel activation voltage, a drug potency, a dose, an in-vivo selectivity, or a clinical effect (all [O] with stated obstacles). The felt/affective pain is the Felt Cognition volume\u2019s. No molecule is designed and nothing diagnoses, treats, or prescribes.
