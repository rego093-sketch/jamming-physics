Classical and physiological anchors — [L] → [V]/[V-arith]

  • DNA stacking thermodynamics — SantaLucia 1998, a unified view of nearest-neighbour DNA thermodynamics (PNAS 95:1460): the source of the γ method.
  • Cochlear place-map — Greenwood 1990, a cochlear frequency-position function (JASA 87:2592): the ~20 Hz–20 kHz human tonotopy reproduced in tonotopy.
  • Hair-cell gating spring — Howard & Hudspeth 1988; Corey & Hudspeth 1983; Markin & Hudspeth 1995; Martin, Mehta & Hudspeth 2000: the two-state MET switch and negative bundle stiffness.
  • Hopf critical cochlea — Camalet, Duke, Jülicher & Prost 2000; Eguíluz et al. 2000; Hudspeth, Jülicher & Martin 2010: the self-tuned critical oscillator and the cube-root used in the cochlear amplifier.
  • Phototransduction — Fesenko, Kolesnikov & Lyubarsky 1985 (Nature 313:310): cGMP-gated rod conductance, the basis of phototransduction.
  • Vestibular canal mechanics — Steinhausen 1933; Van Egmond, Groen & Jongkees 1949 (J Physiol 110:1); Jones & Milsum 1965: the torsion-pendulum and velocity storage in vestibular balance.
  • Ocular optics — the Gullstrand reduced/schematic eye and Hofstetter 1965 age–amplitude formula, reproduced in ocular optics.

Root-cause treatment literature (2023–2026)

  • AMD / geographic atrophy — pegcetacoplan (C3) and avacincaptad pegol (C5), both FDA 2023; slow lesion growth on an anatomic endpoint, with no functional visual-acuity gain yet (partial).
  • Myopia control — IMI consensus: low-dose atropine, peripheral-defocus optics, outdoor light (retinal dopamine), and repeated low-level 650 nm red light; RCT-supported ≥50% slowing, long-term safety still accruing.
  • Glaucoma — Rho-kinase (ROCK) inhibitors netarsudil and ripasudil/fasudil: restore trabecular outflow plus IOP-independent retinal-ganglion-cell neuroprotection.
  • Presbycusis / SNHL — OTOF gene therapy restored hearing in children (clinical, 2024); ATOH1 hair-cell regeneration remains immature for acquired loss (contested); NT-3/BDNF synapse repair preclinical.
  • Cataract — pharmacological chaperones / oxysterols: reports of reversal were not replicated (Daszynski et al. 2019), so efficacy is unproven and surgery remains standard (contested).
  • Diabetic retinopathy — glycemic control resets the systemic setpoint (root); anti-VEGF blocks the downstream neovascular attractor (complication).
  • BPPV — canalith repositioning (Epley / Semont): mechanically returns the otoconia at the source, an instrument-level root-cause fix.
