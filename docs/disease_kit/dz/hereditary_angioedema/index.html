tranexamic acid○ novel leaddir: decrease · approved
Class. antifibrinolytic (plasmin inhibition; lowers contact-system / kallikrein activation)
Mechanism. Blocks plasminogen activation and plasmin activity, dampening plasmin-mediated activation of the factor XII / kallikrein contact system and so lowering bradykinin generation -- the mediator whose unchecked production drives angioedema attacks when C1-inhibitor (SERPING1) is deficient. Direction: decrease / oppose the bradykinin-generating drive. Allele scope: agnostic -- acts on the contact system downstream of the SERPING1 lesion. Pathway-specific to the kallikrein-bradykinin axis (mapped to hereditary angioedema). A very-low-cost agent, in contrast to C1-inhibitor concentrates and the branded kallikrein-targeting biologics.
Prior-art. Tranexamic acid is approved as an antifibrinolytic but NOT for hereditary angioedema; it is a guideline-supported off-label long-term-prophylaxis option (not first-line). Direction-only, low-cost lead -- versus high-cost C1-inhibitor concentrates and kallikrein biologics.
Safety (qualitative; no magnitude). post-marketing safety profile on record (approved antifibrinolytic for bleeding; used off-label / guideline-supported as low-cost long-term prophylaxis in hereditary angioedema, not first-line, not HAE-approved; qualitative; no magnitude)
