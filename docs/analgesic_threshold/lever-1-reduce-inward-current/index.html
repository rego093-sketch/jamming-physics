What this lever does

Lever 1 lowers the inward depolarising current (Na⁺, Ca²⁺, or proton/ATP-gated) so the pain fibre needs a stronger stimulus to fire. The promoter reads place each gene on the firing-threshold scale; the direction of intervention is anchored to cited agents such as the FDA-approved Na_V1.8 blocker.
