Free to reuse

This map is free to reuse — including commercially — under CC BY 4.0, to lower the cost and broaden the search for non-opioid analgesics.

  • License. CC BY 4.0. Reuse, adapt, and build on the reads and the lever map with attribution; commercial use is permitted.
  • Reproduce. Every read and gate regenerates deterministically (offline, drift 0) from public promoter sequence: clone https://github.com/rego093-sketch/jamming-physics and run repro/run_all.py.
  • What you get. 27 graded target reads, a three-lever direction of intervention, a burden-weighted prioritisation, an L3-honesty gate, and a precision local-anaesthesia map — each target a separate page.
  • What you must supply. All clinical magnitudes — potency, selectivity in vivo, efficacy, safety, formulation, dose — are [O] open here and remain your own scientific, ethical, and regulatory responsibility.
  • Contact / cite. Young Jae Lee, ORCID 0009-0002-7535-8245. A Zenodo DOI is pending and will be inserted on publication.
