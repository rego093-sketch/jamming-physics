## How to incorporate a NEW volume (the lossless pipeline)
1. Author the paper per `VP_SPEC_v1_9.md` (template §6); register its row in `registry/vp.manifest.json`.
2. If it introduces new shared terms/modules, add them to `registry/concepts.json` / `modules.json` first.
3. `python3 tools/build_decls.py`   → writes its `_decl.json` (and refreshes all).
4. `python3 tools/build_strips.py`  → injects its inherits-strip.
5. `python3 tools/retrofit_cards.py`→ links its dictionary cards.
6. `python3 tools/gate_shared_infra.py <new_id>` → must be REQUIRED PASS + BODY clean before merge.

## Design notes (so future edits don't regress)
- `data-concept` is the **canonical** dictionary id; `data-locked` may be an aka or a local token.
  The gate resolves `data-locked` via the concept aka map and requires `data-concept == canonical(data-locked)`.
- **Volume-local locked quantities** (e.g. geodynamics `omega_nogo`, `phi_jam`) are self-contained cards
  WITHOUT a `/concepts/` link — they are not cross-cutting vocabulary, and the gate exempts them.
- Registries (`concepts.json`, `modules.json`) are LOCK: never hand-edit pages; edit the JSON and regenerate.
- Aggregates (manifest, coverage, homepage modules section, sitemap) regenerate from declarations + registries.
