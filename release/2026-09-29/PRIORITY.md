# Upgrade priority — release 2026-09-29

Order in which to publish the new Zenodo versions. The order is by how much the published record is now out of date:

1. **Major** — a headline or central claim was corrected, retired or found circular, or new decisive experiments exist.
2. **Moderate** — grades relabelled and notable corrections made; the headline is unchanged.
3. **Minor** — wording or metadata only.
