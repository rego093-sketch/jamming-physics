## Summary
VP Theory is the root volume of the VP (Vacuum / Jamming Physics) corpus. It inherits from no other volume and adds one module: the vacuum modelled as a jammed elastic solid at the isostatic point, the identification of light with its single surviving elastic wave (c² = B/ρ), and the constant chain that follows (headline mₚ/mₑ = 6π⁵). The simulation result that shear stiffness vanishes at z ≈ 6 while bulk stiffness stays finite is an independent prediction of the jamming model (five observables, z₀ within about 0.1 of 6), and test E2 links a measured light wavelength to the electron scale at about ±10% (independent prediction, PASS, residual A_req/median = 1.052). The identification "the surviving wave is light" itself is graded [H] and classed as interpretation in the claims ledger (no numeric test of the identification; the surviving mode is longitudinal while light is transverse, and the E/B lattice run is data-pending). The mass ratio mₚ/mₑ = 6π⁵ is graded [F | LOCK-NU-N] and classed as anchor restatement (selected discrete parameters, residual −18.8 ppm kept [O], look-elsewhere chance about 4×10⁻⁴). The R19 switch normal form is not part of this volume; it is first stated in the dna volume.
